CHICAGO, AUG. 13-Lanky
Erik Johnson of Highland Park,
Ill., and underdog Dan Moor-
head of suburban Elmhurst
captured 25-lap NASCAR late
model stock car main events
here at O'Hare Stadium Friday
and Saturday nights.
Johnson, sidelined with
mechanical problems Saturday
night, won the big race Friday
to continue his advance into the
roll of top threat at O'Hare. He
was trailed by Freddy Loren-
zen of Elmhurst and Chicagoan
Roy Czach. All three were
wheeling 1957 Chevrolets.
In racking up his fourth main
event win of the year, John-
son had the Martinelli Bros. en-
try perking in top form even
though Lorenzen committed a
mental miscue in assuming that
Johnson was a lap behind. He
crossed the finish line about
three length behind the lanky
Swede.
Bob Weyrauch continued his
impressive streak in the Cadet
division when he won the 25
lapper for that group in a '57
Chevy. Another top contender,
Dick Erickson of Chicago, was
the victim of a spectacular flip
down the front straightaway on
the first lap. He suffered a cut
lip.
