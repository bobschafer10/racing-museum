Billy Johnson First in
Fair Park's 100-Lap Fea-

As Car Burns . . . Johnson,
Biertzer Tie for 1960 Track

7,300 Is Season's High . . .
All Time Record Purse of
$5,115 Recorded . . . Drag-
sters Stage Speed Run
Demonstrations . . . Brylski
Wins Roll-Over Contest. ..
Those are the headline high-
lights of Thursday night, Aug-
ust 11, as the Fair Park Speed-
way ended its 1960 season with
a gigantic 12 event program.
Fans were treated to some 180
laps of racing in all, and saw
Oconomowoc's ace modified
stock car pilot and former mid-
get auto racing champion, Billy
Johnson, Jr., capture his first
modified victory in winning the
100-lap grand championship
season finale modified feature
race.
The win moved the 36-year-
old speedster into a dcad tie
for the 1960 track champion-
ship. Johnson and Etchie Biert-
zer, Barton, Wisconsin, will
reign as co-champions of the
Fair Park Speedway until next
year.
A crowd of some 7,300 per-
sons, one of the largest paid
crowds in many a year, con-
tributed towards an all-time
record prize purse amounting
to $5,115. The previous high
was recorded in August of 1950,
when the payoff amounted to
$4,995. Johnson's share
of
Thursday
night's
money
amounted to over a 10 per cent
slice of the record amount.
Bill Schwister of Milwaukee
was perhaps the most unfortun-
ate driver of the night. For the
second year in a row, the 100-
lap grand championship run
can aptly be called the "Heart-
break Hundred."
Fans will recall that last year
on August 13, Schwister had
won the event, taking over on
the 68th lap, when leader Ed-
die Stillman of Milwaukee was
forced out with mechanical
troubles, and another top-run-
ning favorite, Miles Melius,
Slinger, ran out of fuel after
an earlier accident had punc-
tured his fuel tank. Stillman at
that time had built up a size-
able lead and Melius had un-
lapped himself and was only
1/8th of a lap from taking over
the lead on the 74th lap when
his fuel tank ran dry.
