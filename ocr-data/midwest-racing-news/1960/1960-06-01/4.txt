Race Program Washed Out
After Four Events:
Waukegan Speedway Victim of Weather Once Again
Trying hard to beat the impending rain, the Waukegan
speedway abandoned all hopes for at least a half-way successful
race program, when a heavy downpour hit the grounds during
the start of the semi-feature event.
The-entire program was staged on a touch and go situation
all night:as rains came intermittantly throughout the program.
Time trials began at 7:15 but soon a shower dampened the track
sufficiently to call a halt to the activities. When the showers
stopped many of the cars and numerous tow trucks took to the
quarter/mile oval to try and pack it down so the show could
go on.
The process was again repeat-
ed and then as time progressed
a meeting was held among the
50 some drivers waiting to help
put on the holiday weekend
racing program. It was here
that drivers decided to draw
for positions in the heat races
as qualifying trials could no
longer be staged because of the
time element. In between brief
showers the 10 lap events were
held, but at the start of the
semi, the he avy downpour
forced eancelation of the night's
activities and sent home some
2,000 disappointed spectators
who had already braved the
wet and threatening weather.
Because four complete events
were run off, the program was
considered complete and rain
checks cannot be honored next
week.
The four heats that were held
provided plenty of excitement
