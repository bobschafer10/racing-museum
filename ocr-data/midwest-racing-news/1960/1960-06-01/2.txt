CEDARBURG, May 30-Na-
tional Champion Carroll Res-
weber set three track records
.Sunday as he captured the ex-
pert championship in the 23rd
Annual Memorial Day motor-
cycle races. The races are spon-
sored by the Milwaukee Motor-
cycle Club.
Resweber turned in his third
record breaking performance
before a crowd of 4,700 when
he sped to victory in the expert
finals with 59.404 mph average.
His average of 59.818 also es-
tablished a heat record as did
his 61.64 in the qualifying lap.
The amateur crown went to
Bud Schultz, Dearborn, Mich.,
while Ed Clifford of Greenfield
captured the novice title.
Schultz's time was 5:33.40 and
Clifford's time was 5:39.75.
The amateur final had to be
restarted when three riders
spilled in the first turn of the
second lap. They were Roger
Jackson of Kenosha, Ch uck
Landers of Chicago and Leo
Fortune, Webster City, Ken-
tucky. No one was injured.
The expert final saw some
excitement as Tom Gates of
Elmhurst, Ind., and Larry Stone
of Detroit battled for third po-
sition in the 5th lap. Gates tan-
gled with Stone and spilled, but
neither of the drivers was hurt.
Al Berk of Minneapolis was
running in second position dur-
ing the 8th lap with only 2 laps.
