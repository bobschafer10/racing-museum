UNION GROVE, May 30-
Better than 300 stock and com-
petition cars were entered in
the Memorial Day drag event
at Union Grove. The rain out
on Sunday didn't dampen the
fans' spirits, as they turned out
in full force for Monday's event.
The highlight of the meet was a
run made by Pete Farnsworth.
His Chrysler powered fuel drag-
ster turned an et of 9.80 and a
speed of 169.81 mph on seven
cylinders. On a previous run
which netted him a 160 mph,
Pete blew two head gaskets,
was only running on six cylin-
ders, at three-quarters open
throttle.
Del Fisher, driving the "Hall
Chevrolet Special" owned by
Mike Marenoff of Milwaukee,
worked furiously to get the
bugs out of his car. When the
work was completed, however,
he took the little eliminator at
a 12.26 et and a speed of 116.88
mph. Del's car is a 1955 Chev-
rolet with a "GMC 6-71 Blow-
ed", "Hilbourn injection" and
an "Isky-cycle cam". This car

One of the sharpest appearing drag cars at
Union Grove, Wis., is this 1931 Chevy coupe. The .
car is jointly owned by the gentlemen shown in
the photo who are (left to right) : Dick Otto, Mil-
waukee; Niel Hoffman, Thiensville; Art Boeck,
Milwaukee; Ross Balsom, Milwaukee; Ed Wink-
ler, Milwaukee; Chuck Perault, Milwaukee, and
Ron Braden, Milwaukee.
The car is powered by a 1956 Corvette engine,
with a full race cam, using four carburetors. The
car took a first place at a recent Rod & Custom
show.
