Wednesday, September 21, 1960
Joe Kosiske Killed
In Highway Accident
Joe Kosiske of Omaha, Neb.,
master mechanic and owner of
son Bob Kosiske's 1959 T-Bird,
was fatally injured in a high-
way accident while returning
home from a stock car race
meet in Madison, Minn.
This was the second year of
IMCA competition for the Ko-
siske family. Bob is ranked
fourth in current Association
point standings.
The loss of the elder Kosiske
will be sorely felt by members
of the auto race fraternity, not
to mention his fa mily and
friends. Though a keen com-
petitor, Joe was always there
to offer friendly encourage-
ment and a helping hand along
the pit wall.
