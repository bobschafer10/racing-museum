NASCAR
vs. USAC
1959 Indianapolis 500 winner
Roger Ward, who had to settle
for second place this year be-
cause of tire wear, has a prob-
lem and he doesn't know when
and/or if it will be solved . . .
"I'd like to race against Curtis
Turner, Fireball Roberts and
the other top NASCAR drivers
just to see if I can beat them.
I know there are some great
drivers running NASCAR races,
but I'll never know whether
they're better than I am, or
whether I can outrace them
unless I get chance to compete
against them," Ward said dur-
ing a Charlotte, N. C., visit this
week. "Sure, I would race
against them if the two sanc-
tioning organizations, USAC
and NASCAR, could ever get
together . . . and I would also
like to see the NASCAR driv-
ers take a shot in the sprint
and championship cars."
