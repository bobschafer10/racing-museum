Area Race
Fans Soaked
It was indeed a hectic week-
end for area racing fans. Near-
ly 15,000 braved rain to wait
out the weather at the State
Fair Park, in hopes that it
would let up in order to get in
the championship 250 mile late
model stock car race. To no
avail, however, as the weather-
man just wouldn't co-operate
and the rains became harder.
The rains also cancelled the
season finale at the Slinger
Speedway where the modified
stocks were scheduled to go for cellation.

Pabst Passes Test
Augie Pabst of Milwaukee proved he was one of the best
sports car drivers in the country by winning the United States
Auto Club national championship in 1959. Thursday noon the
talented youngster proved he could handle a late model stock
car as he took his driver's test preparatory to Sunday's 250 mile
late model national championship stock car race at State Fair
Park.
Driving the same 1958 Ford
that carried Tony Bettenhau-
sen, Tinley Park, Ill., to vic-
tories in 200 mile races here
this summer, Pabst revealed to
trackofficials.and chief me-
chanic Árt Lamey that he was
right at home in a stock car,
the first time he had ever tried
his hand at this type of racing.
Pabst turned in nearly 50
miles of practice. The first 15
miles were practice and warm-
up laps. On his 16th mile he
turned the track in 41.41 sec-
onds. By the 28th mile, he had
whittled the time to 40.57 sec-
onds. His fastest lap was his
36th mile which he turned in
40.18 seconds (89.59 miles per
hour). One track official re-
