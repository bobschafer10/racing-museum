ALMOST $100,000
The total prize monies paid out to drivers that
have competed at the four tracks operated by Midwest
Speedways is rapidly approaching the $100,000 mark.
The tracks included in this total are Wisconsin Fair
Park, Hales Corners, Cedarburg and Waukegan. The
one special program at the O'Hare Stadium in Chicago
is also included.
The total now stands at an official $96,024.53.
With weather conditions favorable for the last race
events planned, the $100,000 goal may be reached. The
sum is believed to be the largest in the country for any
one organization engaged in the promoting of quarter-
mile stock car racing. The prize point fund being with-
held for the end of the season awards are included in
this total.
