KRIEGER SETS
ONE LAP RECORD
By Jim Engel
Saturday night, Sept. 17 -
Saturday night action at the
Hales Corners Speedway pro-
vided fans with an abundance
of racing thrills. Edwin "Et-
chie" Biertzer of Barton, Wis.,
won the 50-lap championship
feature and Greg Krieger, Mil-
waukee, set a new one lap track
record in qualifying runs.
The 50- lap win was an im-
portant one for Biertzer as he
earned 300 points towards his
season championship standings.
Those 300 points, coupled with
the 55 points he earned for be-
ing the third fastest qualifier.
moved him 10 points ahead of
Miles Melius of Slinger, Wis.
Biertzer had trailed Melius by
345 points going into the eve-
ning's action, and his 10 point
lead was earned when Melius
failed to appear because of ap-
parent car trouble.
Feature action was stopped
twice because of track acci-
dents. The first occurred on the
fifth lap when Linus Peters,
West Bend, spun out and
caused an eight car entangle-
ment which completely blocked
the west turn. Norbie Klemp,
Fredonia, who had been lead-
ing from the first lap, again
took the lead on the restart and
held it until the ninth when
he spun completely around. He
got immediately back into the
race, dropping to second place
