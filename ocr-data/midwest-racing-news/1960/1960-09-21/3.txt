Beaver Dam Sets
Season Finale
The season finale modified
stock car program at the Beav-
er Dam Raceway is scheduled
to go Monday night, Sept. 26,
with a championship 50 lap
feature race on tap. A beauti-
ful trophy will be awarded the
winner in addition to the regu-
lar prize money.
The forty fastest, modifieds
and/or stocks will qualify for
the four heats, semi and 50 lap
run. Non-qualifiers will again
be allowed to compete in a
special bonus race attraction.
Expected to head the field
will be Miles Melius, Slinger,
and Etchie Biertzer of Barton.
In the three previous modified
programs, Melius has cleaned
house on two occasions and
Biertzer took the fast time and
won the feature on the last run.
In the event of rain, track
promoter Hal Woods has set
up the rain date for the fol-
lowing Monday night, Oct. 3.
Time trials will start at 7
p.m. with the first event sched-
uled for 8:15 (Central Standard
time). The Beaver Dam Race-
way is located just a half mile
east of Beaver Dam on High-
way 33.
