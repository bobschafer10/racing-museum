SUPERSTITIOUS auto race
drivers take note: Buck Baker,
the former national late model
champion of Spartanburg, S.C.,
has felt for years that permit-
ting his picture to be taken
before a race was a bad omen.
He all but refused to pose with
a beauty queen at the Myrtle
Beach, S.C. 100-miler, consent-
ing only after her persuasion.
It happened. Baker won the
race, his first of the year. .. .
"Even so, don't think for a
minute I'm going to break any
other superstitions like driv-
ing a green car," declares
Baker. .  . Lee Petty, defend-
ing national champion out of
Randleman, N.C., gave his sec-
ond son, Maurice, his first race
ride at Birmingham, Ala., last
week and Maurice rewarded
with an eighth place finish. ..
Putting Maurice in a car to
join the other Petty son, Rich-
ard, gives the Petty's the only
three-member family team in
NASCAR racing.
