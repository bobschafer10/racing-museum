Nelson
Strategy Pays
Off as Leaders
Falter
By Al Krause
MILWAUKEE, WIS., AUG.
21-Norm Nelson, Racine, Wis.,
thrilled a crowd of more than
17,000 here today as he won the
150 mile national championship
late model stock car race here
on the one mile paved track.
The race, one of the most thrill-
ing in recent years, kicked off
the four days of racing here
during state fair week. Nelson
averaged 86.393 miles per hour,
which was not a record.
Nelson's 1960 Ford owned by
the Zecol-Lubaid Co. of Mil-
waukee, performed well all
through the event. The Great
Dane started fourth but
dropped back to eighth place,
then slowly came back to take
other cars and by 70 miles was
in third spot. When Rodger
Ward dropped out on the 127th
mile, Nelson grabbed second
place and was six seconds be-
hind in the late stages of the
event.
Nelson also picked off the
National Racing Affiliates tro-
phy in his victory. Earlier in
the day in time trials, Paul
Goldsmith, St. Clair Shores,
Mich., shot his 1960 Pontiac
around in 39.57 seconds (90.7
mph). Goldsmith tore into the
lead followed by Ward but un-
like his July 24 ride, never
did get much of a lead.
Ward tailed Goldsmith and
was soon joined by Tony Bet-
tenhausen. Those three ran in
that order until Bettenhausen
made a quick stop at the 80
mile mark. Coming out of the
north turn on the 33rd mile,
Ward shot around Goldsmith
and then held onto the lead
until he pitted on the 106th
mile for gas. Goldsmith then
took the lead which he held for
34 more times around before
a stop for fuel and a right front
Nelson, driving a calculated,
cool race. then moved to the
front and finished 51 seconds
in front of runnerup Les Snow,
Bloomington, Ill., in a 1960
Pontiac. Arnie Gardner, Ba-
tavia, Ill., making his second
start in a race here, finished
fourth behind Goldsmith.
Gardner drove the race with
his left hand in a cast which
had been broken in a short
track spill some two weeks
ago. Hard telling what he
might have done with two
hands and the fact that he spun
on the 10th mile and still kept
going.
A total of 19 laps (miles)
was run under the yellow flag.
