Victory
Shades Krieger Before 3,150 Fans
By Jim Engel
WAUKEGAN, ILL., Sunday, August 21- Before the sec-
ond largest crowd of the season, a former Waukegan champion,
broke into the winner's circle once again as he captured the
25 lap main event for modified stocks before 3,150 fans. His
win marked his first feature victory since the track's reopen-
ing back in 1957. Bob Karry, formerly of Waukegan and now
residing in Lake Villa, Ill., captured the win in fine fashion
as he shaded Milwaukeean Greg Krieger by less than a car
length at the finish line. He averaged 50.28 mph in doing so,
and rcorded the fastest feature time so far this year. His speed
was just a shade off the all time record of 50.45 mph set last
year by Bob Ratajczyk. Karry has been a former Waukegan
Speedway champion several times back in the early 1950's.
