Letters To The Editor
Contributions to this column will be considered only if name,
address and phone number of the sender is enclosed, not neces-
sarily for publication but as evidence of good faith. Items should
be brief and of general interest to all readers. Send all material
to Midwest Racing News, c/o Editor, 6127 West Blue Mound
Road, Milwaukee 13, Wisconsin. Names will be withheld upon
request for bonafide reasons, subject to the discretion of the
editor. Phone numbers will not be printed in any case.
Dear Sir:
I have been to every race at Hales Corners this year, and
haven't seen some of last year's drivers. Could you tell me if
Paul Muehl, Russ Knapton and Don Schuppel are still racing or
have they retired from racing.
D. Eckert:
Paul Muehl has not had a car to run this year and is keeping
busy as sales manager for Hiller Ford in Hales Corners. He may
return to racing in 1961, but it is not definite.
Russ Knapton, a top runner in modified racing a few years
back, was also without a car this year. His occupation takes
him out of town quite frequently, and he has not been at the
tracks very often. He ran at Waukegan two weeks ago piloting
Gordy Gorman's number 61 to a fourth place in the semi feature.
However, a car is being built for him at the present time, and
it is expected he will be making his first appearance in his
number 17 some time this week.
We have been told that Don Schuppel has gone into the
construction business and his time has been completely taken
up, leaving no time for racing. The car he started building, for
this season, he has sold to Cy Fell of Lomira, and is now being
driven by Ken Tlougan. Schuppel may be back in racing next
year if time permits him to do so. He competed in one race
at Cedarburg this year, driving Jim Mitchell's number 54 and
placed 12th in the feature event.
