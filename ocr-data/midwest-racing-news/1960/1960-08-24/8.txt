One of the Midwest's fastest
and most well known fuel
dragsters made its first ap-
pearance on Sunday, Aug. 21,
at the Great Lakes dragaway,
Union Grove, Wis. The famed
"Missouri Missile" piloted by
its new owner and driver, Rod
Stuckley from Kansas City,
Mo., put on quite a show, mak-
ing four runs in the 180 m.p.h
mark.
Stuckley acquired the "Mis-
sile" only two weeks ago from
former owner and driver Lou
Cangollose also from Missouri.
Stuckley turned in a very
commendable performance for
having driven the dragster for
so short a time. According to
Stuckley, the dragster was not
performing at peak efficiency,
but with a return engagement
in the offing, he hopes to im-
prove his speed considerably.

Wausau '50' to
Ernie Derr
A rapid field of IMCA late
models took to a perfectly con-
ditioned Wisconsin Valley Fair
Grounds track Saturday night
at Wausau, and proceeded to
establish two new track rec-
ords. Champion Ernie Derr
drove his '57 Pontiac to victory
and a new mark in the 50-lap
main event. His winning time
was 25:11.26.
Another '57 Pontiac driven
by Dick Hutcherson of Koekuk,
Ia., led a stellar field to the
checkered flag in the 10-lap
match race and set a new track
record of 5:5.56. Derr had to be
content with third money in
the match race as Art Brady
of Peoria, Ill., squeeezed in for
second spot in his '57 Chevy.
Brady had previously estab-
lished a new 10-lap mark as he
placed first in the 3rd 10-lap
heat race with a time of 5:11.91.
