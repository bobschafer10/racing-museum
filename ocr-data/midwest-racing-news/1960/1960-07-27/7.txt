Paul Feldner Wins
By Jim Engel
Colgate, Wis., was all smiles as he came home the winner in the
25 lap modified stock car main at the Hales Corners Speedway
Saturday night, July 23, capturing his first major victory in over
three years of racing. 3,600 fans in the stands were treated to
some fine competitive racing in addition to six spectacular roll-
overs, four of which took place in the stock jalopy feature.
Feldner, who just made the
race by posting the 18th fastest
qualifying time in a field of
nearly 50 cars, took full advan-
tage of his front row starting
position and piloted a smooth
and well-calculated race, beat-
ing off late lap challenges of
Fuzzy Fassbender and Miles
Melius, the two ace Slinger pi-

to Fassbender on the l9th lap

Feldner's winning speed was
clocked at 55.62 m.p.h.
