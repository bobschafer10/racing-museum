tires being used by Ward and
Bob Pronger. The left front
tire on Ward's car was not the
same size as the other three,
according to Emil Andres,
USAC Stock Car Director of
Speed. This was in violation of
the terms spelled out in the en-
try blank. Pronger's tires were
found to be of the same size
on all four wheels and he was
restored to fourth place, having
completed 200 miles.
