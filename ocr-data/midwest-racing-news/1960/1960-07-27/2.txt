Wednesday, July 27, 1960
Drivers Sign Up
For Chilean Benefit
(From the American Red Cross
Press Dispatches:)
More than 50 local amateur
drivers have signed up to race
in the big Chilean Relief Bene-
fit jalopy races to be held at
the Waukegan Speedway on
Friday, August 5th, at 8:30 p.m.
The rain date is Aug. 12th.
Speedway operator, Mike
Kaishian, gave racing fans a
sample of what to expect in a
15 lap extra added attraction
at last Sunday night's races.
Twenty-two jalopies of about
1950 vintage lined up on the
track. After 15 thrilling laps,
10 cars were strung from one
end of the track to the other.
Cars collided with other cars,
bounced off the wall or rolled
over. Drivers jockeying for posi-
tion were driving through
spaces that almost looked to
small for a bicycle. In spite of
the collisions, the tracks record
of no serious injuries went un-
marred.
Proceeds from the Aug. 5th
races will go to Lake County's
Chilean Relief Fund. To date,
nearly $5,000 has been collected
in the county. Of this amount,
over $2,200 has come from the
Naval Training Center, $377
from the Naval Hospital, and
$100 from Fort Sheridan. The
goal for this area has been set
at $15,000.
Chilean authorities estimate
that at least a quarter of a bil-
lion dollars will be needed to re-
build Chile. A 400 mile area of
long Chilean coastline was dev-
astateed by floods, earthquakes,
and yolcanic eruptions which
began on May 21. More than
two million persons are home-
fless with over 5,000 dead.
Lake Countians can help
these unfortunate people by di-
rect contributions or by attend-
ing the Chilean Benefit races.
Admission is $1.50 for adults
and 25c for children. Gates open
at 6 p.m., with time trials start-
ing at 7:30 p.m., and races get-
ting underway at 8:30 p.m. The
speedway is located on West
Washington in Waukegan, be-
tween Greenbay Road and the
Skokie Highway.
Drivers from the Milwaukee
and surrounding areas are in-
vited to participate in the jalopy
race program at Waukegan
Speedway. Cars will be limited
to strictly stock cars with roll
bars and the other safety fea-
tures. Besides the time trials,
there will be four heat races,
semi-feature, and a feature,
plus added attractions. The pay-
off will be similar to what was
paid at the last event and what
you are accustomed to running
for in this area. However, tro-
phies will be awarded in each
event. The top 15 qualifiers are
guaranteed $5.00, and all others
guaranteed at least $2.00.
For further information con-
tact Midwest Speedways, Inc.,
6127 West Blue Mound Road,
Milwaukee 13, Wis., phone SP.
4-4748, or Waukegan Speedway,
kegan, Ill., phone MA. 3-9540.
