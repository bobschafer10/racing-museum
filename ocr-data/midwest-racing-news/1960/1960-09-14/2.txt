Rain Hampers Beaver Dam Program
With an off and on drizzle that started about 8 p.m., the
third modified stock car program at the Beaver Dam Raceway
played host to a small, frost-bitten, rain-scattered crowd of just
over 700 persons, who saw Etchie Biertzer of Barton, Wis.,
take the fast qualifying honors and win the feature 30 lap event.
A fifth lap pile up of over
half the field caused the race
to be stopped after Biertzer
had moved up through the field
and was running in second spot.
The entire race was then re-
lined in their original starting
positions and Al Feller, May-
ville, took the lead until about
the midway point when Biert-
zer stormed his way to the lead
and held it for the rest of the
race.
Norbie Klemp, Fredonia,
placed second; Paul Feldner,
Colgate, was third; Billy John-
son, Oconomowoc, took fourth;
Carl Kulow, Plymouth, was
fifth; and Bill Goeden, West
Bend, took sixth. Feller, who
experienced mechanical diffi-
culties, placed 13th.
Joe Doraty, Rio, edged Dick
Duerst, Madison, in taking the
15-lap semi feature. Doraty
and Duerst both won heat
races.
