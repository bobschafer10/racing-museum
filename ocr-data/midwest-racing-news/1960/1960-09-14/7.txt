Drag Strip Sets
Match Race
A special drag race has been
scheduled for Sunday, Septem-
ber 25th at the Great Lakes
Dragaway which will feature a
best of three match race be-
tween Art Malone and his "Lit-
tle Rebel" and Chris Karami-
sines with his "Chizzler." A
host of other top dragsters of
the nation are scheduled to
compete in the event, among
which is the "Missouri Missile"
from Kansas City, Missouri,
and the Pete Farnsworth Spec-
ial from Milwaukee. Both will
compete against the "Chizler"
and the "Little Rebel."
During the Great Lakes Na-

"Chizler" for the top fuel elim-

to tie the "Swamp Rat" for top
speed honors at 187.50 mph.
