Hoosier Hundred
INDIANAPOLIS, IND. - At
least 19 drivers, with one or
more years of experience at the
Indianapolis Motor Speedway,
will bid for starting positions
in the eighth annual Hoosier
Hundred at the Indiana State
Fairgrounds next Saturday,
September 17, when time trials
get under way at 12:30 p.m.
Several capable rookies also
are included among the official
entries who will be shooting at
the track record of 100.167
miles an hour in an effort to
qualify for one of the 18 spots
