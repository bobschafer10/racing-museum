This paper solicits "Letters to the Editor" because it feels
they will help stimulate our readers' interest. There are a few
conditions, however, which must be kept in mind by the writer.
In the past several weeks this paper has received numerous
letters, all pertaining to the operations of individual tracks. The
comments received have been both favorable and unfavorable
and have included all tracks on the modified stock car circuit,
as well as covering track operations at some of the bigger tracks
and some of the lesser known tracks outside of the general
The favorable comments, however, praise one track and, at
the same time, criticize another. This column has not been set
up to unduly favor any one particular track at the expense of
another, nor has it been set up to criticize the various manage-
In this respect, we wish to tell our readers that letters
portraying compliments or expressing grievances of any one
track or its operations, unless it is of general all-around interest,
should be sent directly to that particular track and its manage-
ment and not to this newspaper. Midwest Racing News cannot
Some of the letters received have been very well written,
but serve no purpose except as an emotional outlet for tempo-
rary pent-up animosities. We don't print those letters for that
reason, and we believe their writers are now glad they weren't
In another outlook, nearly 90 per cent of the letters received
were unsigned. This paper feels it should know who writes a
letter that we publish, and, if requested, we will withhold the
name for bonafide reasons. Midwest Racing News can see no
reason why a writer would want to withhold his name or to use
a fictitious name. The writers must have a reason, and, if they
do, it is also a reason why Midwest Racing News shóuld not
