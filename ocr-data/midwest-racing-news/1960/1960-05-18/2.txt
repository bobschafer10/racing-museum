19 Entries Received
For "Rex Mays 100"
Even though the first race
is a month away, race director
Tom Marchesehas received
19 entries for the 11th an-
nual Rex Mays 100 mile nation-
al championship speedway big
car classic on the one mile
paved track, Sunday, June 5.
The drivers are Richard
Red" Amick, Muncie, Ind.;
Jim Pa c kard, Indianapolis;
John Taylor, Terre Haute; Tony
Bettenhausen, Tinley Park, Ill.,
second ranked driver in the na-
tional standings a year ago. A
total of 16 entries were signed
up by Tom Marchese at the
Indianapolis speedway for the
11th annual Rex Mays 100 mile
national championship speed-
way big car classic at State Fair
Park, Sunday, June 5. This
gives Marchese a total of 19
entries so far.
Amick, who will drive the Id-
