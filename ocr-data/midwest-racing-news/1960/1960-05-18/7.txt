'World 600
CHARLOTTE, N. C. - The
world's longest distance stock
car race on a closed course, the
"World 600" at Charlotte Motor
Speedway on May 29th, will of-
fer a total purse of $106,125 to
competing drivers.
Speedway General Manager
Bruton Smith made the an-
nouncement that the final fig-
ure is well above the $100,000
officials first said would be
guaranteed.
-,
First place in the 600-mile
event for late model cars will
be worth $25,650, an all-time
high for stock car racing. The
first place money is more than
half as much as last year's na-
tional champion, Lee Petty of
Randleman, N. C. won during
the entire season. Petty earned
slightly under $50,000 in 1959.
The NASCAR - sanctioned
"World 600" will be run over
the new 1½2-mile Charlotte Mo-
tor Speedway now under con-
struction just north of here.
Time trials to determine the
60 starting positions in the
"World 600" are scheduled for
May 21, 22, 25, 26, and 27.
Already entered in the race
are nationally-known Curtis
turner of Charlotte, N. C. and
Tim Flock of Atlanta, Ga.
Speedway officials reported
that the base for the asphalt
speedway is already being put
down, and that construction on
the concrete homestretch
grandstand is continuing.
Advance tickets are available
at the speedway as well as at
downtown offices, 108 Liberty
Life Bldg., Charlotte, N. C.
