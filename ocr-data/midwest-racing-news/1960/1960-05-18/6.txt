With plenty of reason to smile is Arnold
Krueger holder of a new world record. Arnie
driving his 1955 Chevy, turned in last Sunday, May
15, at Union Grove, Wis., the new record of
13.56 E.T. at 102.27 m.p.h.
Statistics of Arnie's engine consists of Algoun
fuel injection, Isky cam, Jahns pistons, Schiefen
flywheel, and Getz gears. A surprising fact about
the car is that it did not have a blower. Arnie is
married and has one daughter. He is the owner
and operator of his own "Chevy Shop," specializ-
ing in Chevrolets and Corvettes.
