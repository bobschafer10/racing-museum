MIDWEST RACING NEWS
Quarter Midgets
At Milwaukee
STATE FAIR PARK, June 5
-Above the roar of the big car
race on Sunday the little quar-
ter midgets started their races
in the Fair Park. The track's
favorite boy in his 98 Special,
Jeff Dunham from Big Bend,
took the honors. After win-
ning the first heat and the first
semi event, he 'went on to win
the 25 lap feature.
The fastest Class A qualifier
was Jim Dentici. His time was
9.31 seconds or a speed of 19.33
miles per hour on the 1/20 of a
mile dirt track. Jim also won
the second heat, the second semi
event and came in second place
in the feature.
The fastest novice qualifier
was "Mighty Moe" Christensen,
seven years old, and the only
girl on the track. Her time was
9.57 seconds. Terry "The Ter-
ror" Moore won the third heat.
Don Neary won the fourth heat.
The 15 lap consolation was won
by Bobby Peffer who got off to
a good start from his pole posi-
tion and lapped the field to
win the race.
Eight cars lined up for the
main 25 lap feature in which
the following drivers took part:
Jeff Dunham, Jim Dentici, Gary
Lushine, Rick Bentzien, Terry
Moore, Don Neary, Bobby Pef-
fer, and Randy Christensen.
Dunham had a real job to hold
on to his pole position-Dentici
and Lushine were pushing him
all the way.
The novice heats, as always,
provided the thrills of the day.
In the first heat, Jeff Fennig
had the pole, but after a tangle
with Bobby Patzwald, he was
out of the race. Rick Shady
went through to win. Jeff had
the pole again in the second
heat, but after a smash-up with
Bob Burke, who went right over
the side of his car, Jeff was
out for the day.
Bobby Patzwald won the heat.
Gary Schmidt won the third
heat. The novice feature was
won by'Bob Burke. A real bat-
tle for second place went on
between two brothers, Ronnie
and Rick Shady. Only the sec-
ond time on the track, Ronnie
held back and Rick went all the
way to win second place.
