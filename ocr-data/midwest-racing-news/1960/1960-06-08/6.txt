By Bud De Boer
UNION GROVE, WIS., June
5-Well, Sunday seemed to be
the day for setting records and
there were a few of them set
at the Union Grove drag strip.
Pete Farnsworth of Milwaukee,
driving "Tuffy 1" set a new "B
Dragster" record (fuel class).
Pete turned an et of 9.22 and a
speed of 173.07 mph. He backed
this run up with an 9.36 et and
a speed of 169.81 mph. Pete's
car is powered by a 364 cubic
inch Chrysler and he burns
nitro-methane fuel.
. Mike Marinoff of Milwaukee
set a new world "B Gas" record.
The driver of the car is Del
Fisher of Milwaukee. Their
record run was an et of 12.30
and a speed of 121.21 mph.
They backed it up with a run of
118.42 mph and an et of 12.51.
This car is powered by a 301
cubic inch supercharged Chev-
rolet.

=== COLUMN 4 ===
One of the hottest cars in its class this year
at Union Grove, Wis., drag strip, was the Hot Rod
Shop of Milwaukee's 22 B/A, 1934 Ford coupe.
While towing the coupe on the highway, the tow
car lost control and resulted in the rolling of the
dragster. Luckily the engine was not damaged,
although the coupe itself was nearly ruined. The
car was being driven by Del Fischer and had
Bruce Andrews as mechanic. Both are from Mil-
waukee, Wis. The engine is a 1956 blown Chevy,
with a 265 cubic inch displacement.
