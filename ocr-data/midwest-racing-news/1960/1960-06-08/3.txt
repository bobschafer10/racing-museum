Milt Curcio Wins
Fair Park Feature
- Veteran Racine, Wis., driver,
Milt Curcio, captured the 25-
lap feature race at the Fair
Park Speedway Thursday nite,
June 2, averaging 50.50 mph.
Taking the lead on the 14th lap
from Eddie Stillman of Milwau-
kee, Curcio sped on to victory
by battling off Milwaukee driv-

close second.

draw from the event when he
spun out in a brief tangle with
Krieger for the second spot.
Stillman, who was the track's
1959 champion, has failed to
earn any points toward the 1960
championship after two weeks
of racing at the Milwaukee
track.

Lorenzen Unbeatable
At O'Hare Stadium
CHICAGO, June 3-It's still
Fred Lorenzen the unbeatable
here in the NASCAR late model
stocks at O'Hare Stadium. He
made it six out of even main
events tonight.
The two-time USAC National
Champion of the stocks again
had things virtually all his own
way with defending track
champ Bill Lutz of Louisville
again the No. 1, but in vain pur-
suer. Both were wheeling their
regular 1957 Chevrolets.
Lorenzen added further to to-
night's laurels in the very first
competitive event when he
topped the time mark set only
last Monday (Memorial Day)
by Lutz and he made no bones
about it. Fred knocked nearly
four seconds off the Lutz peak
as he turned the six circuits
in a flat 1:30.
Although he started 16th in
the feature, the torrid Elmhurst
speedster took command on lap
8 and was never in danger. At
the finish it was Lorenzen, Lutz,
Roy Czach of Chicago ('57
Chevy), Whitey Gerken of Chi-
cago ('57 Chevy), and Gene
Marmor of Schiller Park ('57
Pontiac).
Lorenzen carted home the
beautiful Collegiate Pennant
trophy which was happily pre-
sented by none other than Pat
Purtell, executive manager of
NASCÁR, in town for the week
end to look over things at
O'Hare and in the midwest.
