Chairman of the 12th annual
Milwaukee Sentinel-Winneba-
goland Outboard Marathon,
June 26, at Fond du Lac, Wis-
consin.
About 200 drivers are expect-
ed to participate in this year's
race. The Winnebagoland course
is 88 miles, with starting and
finishing point at picturesque
Lakeside Park, Fond du Lac.
The Marathon is always
of
super test of the stamina
both drivers and equipment. To
the winners go Milwaukee Sen-

addition, all drivers who finish
the race receive a Dunphy Me-
dallion, presented by the Dun-
phy Boat Corp., and Kiekhaefer
Corp. presents the stunning
Merrill Jensen Award to the
first place class A driver.
The Sentinel-Winnebagoland
Outboard Marathon is strictly
for stock utility outboard run-
abouts, with five classes com-
peting-A, B, C, 36, and D.
Sanctioned by the American
Power Boat Association, .the
race is co-sponsored by the Sen-

Lac Association of Commerce,
