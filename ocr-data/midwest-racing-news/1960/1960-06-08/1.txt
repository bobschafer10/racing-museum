-Moving into the lead on the

annual 100 mile national speed-

A crowd of 26,527 saw Jolly
Rodger grab off the big end of
the $25,350 purse as well as the
Leader Card trophy. It was by
pure coincidence that the tro-
phy for the 100 miler was put
up by Ward's car owner, Bob

picked off by talented Lloyd
Ruby, Houston, Tex., in the #98

one mile paved oval in 35.47
seconds, averaging 101.49 miles
per hour.
Johnny Thomson, Boyertown,
Pa., tooled the Racing Associ-
ates Spl. #3. into the lead at
the drop of the green flag and
looked all the world as though
he would win his second
straight Rex Mays race here.

margin when clutch trouble put
him out on the 72nd mile. Foyt
then went into the lead but
Ward nosed past him going into
the south turn on the 82nd mile
and that sealed the verdict.
