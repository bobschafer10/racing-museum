It's Billy Woods Again
At Sun Prairie Midget Go
By Tom Bloom
SUN PRAIRIE, WIS.-It Was
that old familiar face of Billy
Woods again Sunday night at
the Angell Park Speedway as
he ran off with the 25-lap fea-
ture event of the evening. Billy
looks like he's out to make it
four years in a row as the Bad-
ger Midget champ, consistently
showing the boys what it's like
to see the tail of the black No.
6 Farchaine Special out in front
of the pack.
Mel Kenyon, Davenport, Iowa
ace who was last week's feature
winner, took the runner-up hon-
ors and was moving up fast,
but the hard charging Kenosha
veteran, Woods, had built up
such a commanding lead there
was little hope in catching him
and he went across the finish
line a straight-away ahead of
Kenyon.
Bill Horstmeyer of Madison,
Wisconsin, looked like he was
off to an easy win as he charged
through the pack like he was
shot from a cannon, but after
a few laps his engine went sour
and he was forced to drop back
out of the top money winners.
Engine trouble also forced Owen
Snyder of Janesville back after
he was running in the top two
positions in the feature. So the
Janesville flash was forced to
settle for the number eight spot
in the feature. Steve Orme,
Springfield, Ill., took the third

by Gary Congden of White-

Woods also ran away with the
