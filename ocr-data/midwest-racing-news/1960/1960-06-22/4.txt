Lutz Wins, Sets Mark
In O'Hare Late Model
CHICAGO, June 17-Defend-
ing track champion Bill Lutz of
Louisville, Ky., tonight won the
NASCAR 25-lap main event for
late model stock cars here at
O'Hare Stadium in the record
time of 6:31.48.
It was his first main event
victory of the year. He drove
the Tom's Standard Service
1957 Chevrolet from far back
in the 18-car field to take com-
mand on lap 7. Chicago's Ray
Czach ('57 Chevrolet) tried in
vain to overhaul Lutz, but at
the finish was eight car lengths
back.

order, were Whitey Gerken of
