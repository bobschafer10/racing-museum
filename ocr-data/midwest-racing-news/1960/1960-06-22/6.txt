NASCAR
at Daytona
DAYTONA BEACH, FLA.,
June 20-A new qualifying rec-
ord of better than 152 miles per
hour is being predicted for
Daytona International Speed-
way when the NASCAR late
model stock car speedsters move
southward for the second an-
nual "Daytona Firecracker 250"
event July 4.
Practically all the drivers tak-
ing part in the Charlotte "World
600" race will move south to
Daytona for the summer speed
classic over the world's fastest
speedway and most of the top
performers are expecting to see
Fireball Roberts' present track
qualifying record of 151.556
miles per hour topped.
Practice for the "Firecracker
250" entries will get underway
at the speedway Monday, June
27, with time trials slated for
Saturday, July 2, to determine
the two pole positions for the
Monday race. Other starting
positions will be determined in
the four 25-mile qualifying
races slated for Sunday, July 3.
Some 50 to 75 drivers, includ-
ing all the top ranking NASCAR
stars are expected to compete
in the Daytona holiday classic
that gets underway at 11 a.m.
(Eastern Standard Time) on
Monday, July 4.
Rated among the toughest
entered
competitors already
for the Daytona race will be

1960 Plymouths; Junior John-
son of Ronda, N. C., winner of
the 1960 "Daytona 500" race,
with a 1959 or 1960 Chevrolet;
Fred Lorenzen of Elmhurst, Ill.,
two-time winner of the USAC
