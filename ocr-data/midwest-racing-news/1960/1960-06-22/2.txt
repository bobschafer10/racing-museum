Krieger Scores First
Clean Sweep of Season
Roll Overs, Accidents Spice
Stock Car Show at Waukegan
In an action packed program, Greg Krieger of Milwaukee
out-sped all competitors to score the first clean sweep of the
modified stock car season before 2,700 weather chilled fans at
the Waukegan Speedway Sunday night, June 19th. Several accl-
dents and three spectacular roll overs highlighted the evening's
long and bitter duel for the
third spot. Roy Martinelli, Half
Day, placed fifth and Don Sorce,
West Allis, finished in sixth po-
sition.
Krieger's win marked his sec-
ond consecutive feature at Wau-
kegan and added another 250
points to his point total for the
season's championship. His win-
ning speed was 49.13 mph.
IRVING TAKES SEMI WIN
Paul Coen of Milwaukee, and
Don Koelling of Wheeling, sur-
vived a spectacular accident in
the semi-feature contested at 15
laps, to finish in the sixth and
seventh spots respectively. The
race was stopped on the second
lap when Koelling spun, and
Coen trying to avoid a mishap,
hit the brakes and was bumped
from behind at the same time.
His car somersaulted over one
time with the back end swing-
ing completely over and around
the front, landing right side up
on the back end of Koelling's
machine.
After another restart, Rod
Fuller, Racine, took a com-
manding lead but spun out on
the 10th lap and failed to place
as Ed Irving of Waukegan,
breezed into the number one
spot and took an easy win over
Bill Ansell of Milwaukee and
Glen Eichmann of Lake Zurich.
Irving's speed was 46.44 mph.
Several spin-outs and acci-
dents thrilled the crowd in all
the 10 lap heat races. Koelling
won the first and Ansell won
the second. Jack Radtke aver-
aged a near record 50.70 mph
in taking the third heat over
Darnell. Krieger took the sec-
ond after holding off Karry for
the ten lap distance.
