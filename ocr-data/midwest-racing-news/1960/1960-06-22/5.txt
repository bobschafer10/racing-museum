Resweber Second
In Laconia Event
LACONIA, N. H., June 19
Cedarburg's Carroll Resweber
finished second in the 100-mile
National Motorcycle champion-
ship here Sunday. His competi-
tor for first place was Dick
Mann of El Sobrante, Calif.
Mann set a record of one hour,
42 minutes and 1.94 seconds at
58.8 mph. He had finished sec-
ond in 1958 and in 1958 placed
third. Mann pocketed a total
win of $1,500 by capturing the
first place.
