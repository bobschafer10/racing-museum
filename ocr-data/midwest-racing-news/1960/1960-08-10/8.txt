the trick this Sunday. Driving

Illinois, who has proved him-
self a very capable pilot and
can really move this machine
off the line. Two of the three
wins were made comparatively
easy on the last round for top
eliminator when for the second

low's" A.A. dragster from Mun-
delien, Ill., blew the engine

races. It seems since "Mr. Bee-
low" mounted that powerful
GMC supercharger on his Pon-
tiac he has had some really
tough luck. He has come a

he sticks with it.
