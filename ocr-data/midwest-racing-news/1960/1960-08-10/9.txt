The week end of Aug. 6-7, marks
the first anniversary of Formula
Junior racing in America. Last year
at this time Walt Hangsen, driving an
Italian Stanguellini, ran at Montgom-

of 50 strictly stock jalopies put on a show of spills and chills

fans of the Waukegan speedway. The stock jalopy program was
held at the speedway Friday night, August 5, as a special benefit
race for the Chilean Relief Committee, associates of the Ameri-
can Red Cross, in their effort to raise monies for the disaster
struck citizens of Chile.
Final tabulations are not in
as yet due to a large number
of advance ticket sales still out,
but it is well estimated that the
Committee will receive more
than $1,200 towards their goal.
Dignitaries of South American
countries made their appear-
ance at the speedway during
the program and expressed
their sincere thanks to Lake
Countians for their tremendous
support of the Chilean people.
Special Consuls of Nicaragua,
Paraguay, Panama, The Do-
minical Republic, Argentina,
Peru, and Chile were present.
Mr. Arsene Denoyer, chair-
man of the Chilean Relief com-
mittee, was present and also
expressed his thanks for the
fine support and the co-opera-
tion rendered among all com-
mittee members. Mr. Denoyer
read the assemblage a telegram
he received from Walter Mul-
ler, Chile's Ambassador to the
United States, thanking him,
the committee and the people
for their efforts in behalf of the
people of Chile. Captain J. C.
Ford, commander of the Naval
Center and a committee mem-
ber, was also introduced to the
audience.
