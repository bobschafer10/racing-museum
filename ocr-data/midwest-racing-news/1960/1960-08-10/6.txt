All Race Honors
By VER-BET
Norm Nelson, the Big Dane
from Racine, Wis., not only won
the 25-lap feature attraction for
the modified stock cars at the
Kenosha County Fairgrounds
Speedway at Wilmot, Wis., Sat-
urday night, but won his 10-lap
heat, turned in the fastest qual-
ifying honors (55.00 mph) and
was the first driver of the 1960
season to make it a "clean
sweep." The 16 fastest car fea-
ture had to be restarted when
a 3 car pile-up on the first cor-
ner of the first lap involved
Bay Darnell, Deerfield, Ill.; Don
Sorce, West Allis; and Jack
Radtke, Grays Lake, Ill. (last
week's feature winner). Minor
adjustments had Sorce and
Darnell ready to compete and
crowd enthusiasm requested a
short delay for favorite Jack
Radtke in order that necessary
welding could be completed,
which was done in unbelievable
record time. Gary Sheldon, Be-
loit, Wis., jumped out into the
Lead until the 10th lap, upon be-
lng overtaken by Dick Ranahan,
Round Lake, Ill. Seven power-
ful modifieds were running
neck and neck in one closely
knit pack until the 15th lap
when Nelson pushed his big
Ford powered Crosley into the
drenched finale. The rains
lead, staying there till the rain
washed out what could have
been a record speed, however,
it is doubted if any changes in
final positions were affected.
As they finished: 1. Norm Nel-
son, Racine, 51.44 mph; 2. Bob
Karry, Waukegan, Ill.; 3. Jack
Radtke, Grays Lake, Ill.; 4.
Dick Ranahan, Round Lake,
Ill.; 5. Don Sorce, West Allis.
The maximum limit of 24
modified stock cars in the 15-
lap semi feature was also a
thriller until the very end.
Gene Murdock, Wauconda, Ill.,
was spun around in front of
the grandstand on the 5th lap,
went through the infield fences
and stopped within one foot of
the judges' -stand, being missed
by only inches by the other 23
racing stocks. The finish had
all the officials checking close-
ly as the top 5 crossed the elec-
tric eye. 1. Rod Fuller, Racine,
49.88 mph; 2. Milt Curcio, Ra-
cine; 3. Ed Irving, Waukegan,
Ill.; 4. George Bellegante, Ken-
osha; 5. Russ Knapton, Mil-
waukee.

Letters To The Editor
I have been going to the races now for three years, So far
this year I have attended eleven races -three at Cedarburg,
three at Hales Corners, and four at State Fair. All this time
my favorite, like a lot of other people, is Miles Melius. One
night I decided to do something about my favoritism toward
Meliusso I wrote this little poem. I think it is good and I
hope you will have it read at one or all of the tracks sometime
before the season closes. I know there are many who will enjoy
it. Thank you.
