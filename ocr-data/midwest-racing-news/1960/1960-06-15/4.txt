MIDWEST RACING NEWS
—Staff Photo by Richard DeMoulpied
"World 600
CHARLOTTE. N. C.-Offi-
cials of the new Charlotte Mo-
tor Speedway today emphatic-
ally denied that the "World
600" race on Sunday, June 19,
is a complete sellout.
Speedway General Manager
Bruton Smith said he had heard
E
reports that all of the nearly
40,000 reserved grandstand seats
have been sold. "These reports
are completely false in every
respect," Smith declared.
"As a matter of fact," Smith
continued, "there are a number
of good seats still available in
all four grandstands at Char-
lotte Motor Speedway."
Officials also plan to place a
sizeable block of tickets on sale
the day of the race.
