Driving at a race track in
one's own "back yard" is one
thing, but coming home the
winner in the feature event is
a "thrill all its own." So said
little Jimmy Gish, 23 year old
West Allis youth who copped
the 25 lap main event at the
Fair Park Speedway Thursday
night, June 9th. His win at the
West Allis speed plant marks
his first feature win at any
track for the speedster now in
his third year of racing. Gish
averaged a near record 50.53
mph in taking his win over vet-
eran driver Ken Tlougan, Mil-
waukee.
Gish took advantage of both
a front row position and various
traek accidents that put other
top running favorites out of the
competition. But the win was
no snap as Gish fought the bat-
tle all the way employing all
the skills he has acquired thus
far. For the first 20 laps it was
a nip and tuck battle with Bay
Darnell of Deerfield, Ill., who
had won the opener on May 26.
When Darnell withdrew with
mechanical difficulties, Gish
took the lead and battled off
Tlougan for the remainder of
the event. Bob Ratajczyk, Mil-
waukee, placed third and Etchie
Biertzer of Barton took fourth
and Jack Frost. Zion, placed
fifth.
Several track accidents put
Miles Melius, Slinger, Milt Cur-
cio, Racine, Bill Schwister, Mil-
waukee, Greg Krieger, Milwau-
kee, and others out of the run-
ning for top honors. Some were
forced out completely.
Gary Sheldon of Beloit won
the 15 lap test averaging 48.70
mph to finish first ahead of
Eddie Stillman, Milwaukee, and
Bill Ansell of Milwaukee. Two
accidents marred the race. A
nine-car pile-up on the first
turn stopped the event when
the track was made impassable.
RESWEBER TAKES
NINTH STRAIGHT
YORK, PA.-National Cham-
pion motorcycle driver, Carroll
Resweber of Cedarburg, Wis.,
won his ninth straight race here
Sunday on the York five mile
dirt track in 4 minutes 52.06
seconds. He won over Bates
Molyneaux of Detroit.
