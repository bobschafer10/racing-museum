MIDWEST RACING NEWS
INDIANAPOLIS, JUNE 7 -
The U. S. Auto Club ruled com-
plaints that record -breaking
rookie race driver Jim Hurtu-
bise drove recklessly in the 100-
mile Milwaukee event last Sun-
day are unfounded.
Henry Banks, USAC director
of competition, said he had "a
real nice chat" with Hurtubise
and was convinced the driver
was innocent of the charges.
Banks explained that it was the
first time Hurtubise drove on
the Milwaukee track and it
wasn't familiar to him. He is
convinced if the rookie got in
the way of some of the other
drivers it was not intentional.
Banks is quoted as saying,
"Jim promised me he would try
to be more careful in the future.
And, of course, he's out there
to win. I think he will be one
of our greatest drivers before
long."
