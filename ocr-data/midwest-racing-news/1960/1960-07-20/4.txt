1960 looks like it'll be the
biggest for Norm Nelson.
The veteran Racine, Wis., late
model stock car racer who has
finished among the top 10 on
six different occasions in the
national point standings for late
models, is having his best year
to date. Nelson finished second
to Don White, Keokuk, Iowa, in
a 300-lap race on the one-half
mile paved track at Nashyille,
Tenn., recently. Two weeks ago
Nelson drubbed White and the
rest of the field in a 200-lap
race at Columbus, Ohio.
Nelson, who was born Jan.
30, 1923, at Racine, broke into
racing in the midget cars in
1941 at Horlick Stadium. He
drove most of the 1942 season
until the war time ban called
a halt to racing. After three
years of service in an army ord-
nance battalion, Nelson returned
to midget racing in 1946. He
suffered the only major acci-
dent of his racing career shortly
thereafter when he turned over
n a midget race at Crown Point,
Ind.
Nelson, whose activity in mid-
gets was a necessity, admitted
that he felt uneasy in the small
cars as he had difficulty wedg-
ing his six foot, two inch, 190
pound frame in the cars. He
turned to hot rod and modified
stock car racing in 1949. On
Aug. 2, 1949, he was the first
man to turn over in a stock car
race on the one-quarter mile
dirt track at the fair park.

Nelson is tied with Marshall

transmission of his 1951 Olds.
In 1955, Nelson beat out Teague

car race. After taking the check-
ered flag, Nelson's right front
tire blew, sending his 1955
Chrysler 300 into the south turn
wall, a totally-wrecked automo-

and walked back to the starting
line to claim the winner's tro-
phy.
In September, 1958, Nelson
won the 250 miler at the fair
park. Smart driving to conserve
his equipment paid off as he
took the lead on the 228th mile.
It was the biggest win of his
career. In 31 stock car races at
the fair park since 1948, Nel-
son's earnings have amounted
to nearly $22,000. He ranks

Nelson has no superstitions
concerning racing, is even tem-
pered and offers no excuses
when he loses a race. To keep
in shape, Nelson has a modified
stock car which he races in the
area. Won three main events
at the fair park last summer and
this past week won the 30-lap

and that he can go all the way
