Point Fund Established at Midwest's Tracks
Point standing at the four tracks operated by Midwest
Speedways will have a special meaning this year, as the top
30 men at each track will share in a specially created driver's
prize point fund at the end of the racing season.
The special fund, was set up at the beginning of the racing
season at each track and has continued along with added monies
going towards the fund each race night. Prize purses each night
are based on 40 per cent of the gate after admission taxes. Of
this 40 per cent 7½/2 per cent is held for the fund, this amount
being equal to 3 per cent of the gate after taxes.
