to his first feature victory of the season at the Fair Park Speed-
way Thursday night, July 14, and took the 30 lap main event for

recall that last week Nelson was just one-half lap away from

Coen could well have swung out
and continued in the race, leav-
ing Peters stalled on the main
chute: Instead he pushed the
disabled machine all the way
around the turn and down the
back chute, and just about the
time they would have reached
the pit area Aaron Solsrud,
Brookfield, rammed into Coen's

cars and forcing them from the
event. The impact gave Peters
the extra distance needed, to
coast into the pit area, and
Coen's and Solsrud's cars were
then left stalled on the track
for the remainder of the event.
Doug Bandl of Milwaukee
took over the lead on lap num-

waukee and went on for the
win averaging 48.88 mph.
The fourth heat race provided
fans with more racing thrills as

bumper battle for first place. On
the fifth lap Ansell spun out,
causing the followers to brake
hard to avoid a collision. The
field was slowed, but Milt Cur-
cio of Racine broke into the
lead and went on for the win
without further mishap.
Several other spin outs and
accidents occurred in all events,
but the two races for the strict-
