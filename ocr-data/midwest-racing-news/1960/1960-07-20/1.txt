=== COLUMN 1 ===
Five
Roll-Overs
By Jim Engel
Before the season's second
largest crowd of some 4,300
fans, Miles "The Mouse" Melius
of Slinger came home the victor
in Saturday night's 25-lap main
event for modified stocks, and
thus became the first driver of
the 1960 season to duplicate a
feature win at Hales Corners.
His winning speed was 55.82
mph. The crowd contributed to-
wards another large purse of
$2,235.
Five roll-overs and numerous
spins and crashes added to the
evening's excitement, but
drivers were injured.
Melius, starting near the end
of the 18 car field, almost lost
out on the third lap when, on
the back stretch, Paul Feldner,
Colgate, spun out, causing a
brief, but precarious, entangle-
ment. Feldner was in turn hit
by Ken Tlougan of Milwaukee.
Melius, in trying to avoid the
accident, swung in, hitting
Tlougan's car slightly, but
enough to force Melius' car up
on two wheels. In this position
he rode out the action, maneu-
vered around the stalled ma-
chines, settled down on all four,
and continued on.
Home-town running mate,
Fuzzy Fassbender, who had
lead the event from its start,
succumbed to Melius' challenges
on lap 16 and went on to settle
for second place. Greg Krieger
of Milwaukee, the current Hales
Corners' point leader, finished
third, after posting the night's
fast qualifying time at 59.94
mph, to tie Billy Johnson's one
lap record set June 4th of this
year.
Paul Coen, Milwaukee, was
the victim of a spectacular 434
turn flip during the running of
the second heat race. Larry
Banach of Milwaukee, spun out
and was hit by Gordy Jutz, Troy
Center, and in the ensuing me-
lee Coen was "tapped" and over
he went. Other spins and crash-
es in the heat event allowed

from lap two of the semi to beat
off challenges from Doug Bandl,
Milwaukee, and Ron Luedtke,
Lomira, to take the 15 lap win
at 51.83 mph.
