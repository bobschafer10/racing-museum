Fastest qualifier of the night,
Horstmeyer started out in the

hardly one-third over. His per-
formance at the wheel of No.

So far this season, Wood has
had difficulties in getting his
ride, the Farchione Special from
Milwaukee and the first brand-
new Kurtis midget to appear
here in several seasons, to han-
dle as he would like, but last
Sunday the shiny, black No. 6
was getting around the three-
tenths oval in fine fashion. He
simply couldn't get around the
masterful Horstmeyer to stay,
although he did poke a nose
past the Madison charger twice,
only to be outgunned down the
chutes.
Another fin e performance
was turned in by Keith Thomas
of Madison in the No. 82 Dari-
Kool Special, who finished a
close third after starting inside
Horstmeyer at the back of the
pack. But Tommy drew little
of the credit he deserved be-
cause few in the crowd-even
the owners and pit crews of the
other cars - could take their
eyes off the terrific hassle be-
tween the leaders.
