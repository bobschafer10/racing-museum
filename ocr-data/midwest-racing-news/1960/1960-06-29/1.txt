By Al Krause
MILWAUKEE, WIS., June 26
With a pile of smashed speed
records in their wake, Parnelli
Jones, Gardena, Calif. and Don
Branson, Champaign, Il. won
the pair of 50 mile sprint car
races here today. Jones, driving
the Fike Plumbing Chevy hum-
bled the 22 car starting field in
the first 50 miler after setting a
new one mile record of 34.202
seconds (105.2 miles per hour).
Branson came back to win in
the Bob Estes car in the second
50 miler and claimed the Jim
Groh Motors trophy on the basis
of total elapsed time, besting
Jones by three seconds.
In the first 50 miler, Jones
jumped off the pole position
and won the race pretty much
as he pleased. Branson was
never worse than second but
was pushed by Elmer George,
Indianapolis, later by A. J. Foyt,
Houston, Tex. George, Foyt,
Tony Bettenhausen, and Jim
Packard duelled throughout the
first 50 miler and were running
third through sixth until Pack-
ard spun in the north turn.
Jones, who also picked off
the Jim Groh Motors trophy as
the fastest qualifier, ran off and
hid at the start of the first 50
miler and would have set rec-
ords but for the 15 miles lost to
the caution flag in three spins,
all in the north turn. Johnny
Thomson, Boyertown, looped
the Stearley car on the eighth
mile, and six miles later, Jim
Hurtibise, Lennox, Cal. spun the
Barnett Chevy in almost the
same spot. Then on the 44th
mile, Packard spun and left the
field with only two miles of
green flag racing in the first
event. Nobody was injured and
all but Packard's car were out
for the day. Hurtibise made a
hurried trip to the pits and re-
placed Gene Hartley in the
John Fray Offy on the 31st mile
and finished 18th.
Foyt looked real good nailing
down fourth and gave Elmer
George a workout for third spot.
George demonstrated that his
year layoff had lost him none
of his skill and cunning. Leon
Clum, the little Wapakoneta, O.
speedster charged through the
pack and was fighting for third
spot in the Cutzwiller Chevy
