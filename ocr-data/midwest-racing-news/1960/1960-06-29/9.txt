Record Crowd Sees
Darnell Win Feature
By VER-BET
Bay Darnell, Deerfield, Ill.,
romped home the winner of the
25 lap modified stock car fea-
ture at Wilmot Saturday night
before a record crowd of 4,320
people who contributed to an-
other record drivers purse of
$1,825. Darnell's Corvette pow-
ered Ford got the workout of
its life the last two laps as Bob
Karry, Waukegan, Ill. (2nd
place) and Bob Rataczyk, Ke-
nosha, Wis. (3rd place) closed
the gap and as the checkered
flag came down it appeared
that there would be three win-
ners instead of one. The lead
was also challenged by Carl
Jensen, Mukwonago, Wis., who
came in 4th and Don Sorce, Mil-
waukee, Wis., who slid across
the finish line for the 5th place.
'Lil' Ed Stillman, Milwaukee,
Wis., and Jack Radtke, Gray
Lake, Ill., encountered fuel mix-
ture problems in the 16 fastest
car feature and Ken Tloughan,
Milwaukee, Wis., with Milt Cur-
cio, Racine, Wis., were forced
out due to gear box failures.
Feature time, 50.81 mph.
Thirty-three modified stock
cars qualified for the 15
lap Semi-Feature, however,
only 21 managed to get the
green starting flag. The huge
crowd howled with amazement
when "James Camerron, Keno-
sha, Wis., went thru the west
end track guard rail, traveled
some 40 feet more and then
went thru the steel protective
park rail another 30 feet out
into a swamp area. No injuries
and only minor damage to the
car but how to get Camerron's
car back into the infield after
the races were completed pre-
sented quite a problem. Roger
Irving, Waukegan, was 1st;
Cookie Alby, Waterford, Wis.,
2nd; Darv Nissen, Zion, Ill., 3rd.
Time, 47.84 mph.
The fastest qualifier for the
evening's competition was Norm
Nelson, Racine, Wis., in his new
high powered Ford (Crosley
body) with 52.16 mph.
The four 10 lap heats for the
modifieds and the special 10
lap heat (reverse direction) for
the strictly stock amateur driv-
ers were as thrill packed as
any racing fan could care for.
Fender bending, rollovers, fence
busting and speed were there in
every event.

DAYTONA BEACH
Climaxing the program will
be the second annual "Daytona
Firecracker 250" NASCAR late
model stock car race on Mon-
day, July 4, with trials July 2
and four 25-mile qualifying
races Sunday, July 3.
More than a hundred drivers
are expected to take part in the
sports car and late model stock
car races at the speedway dur-
ing the two different type race
programs.
Heading the early list of en-
tries announced by Bill Ben-
cker of Winter Haven, chairman
for the sports car race, will be
Roger Penske of Allentown, Pa.,
with one of the new Porsche
RS60's. Porsches are expected
to play a major role in the
sports car championships this
year and Penske is entering the
regional race to get additional
practice over the course that
will be the scene of the final
SCCA nationals in November.
Four Clearwater drivers are
already entered, these including
Bill Story with a Lotus, John
Hoffman with a Renault Coro-
velle, Berth Wright with a Ren-
ault Dauphine and George Peck
with a DB Special.
Four Ft. Lauderdale drivers
are also included among the
early entries. These are Bob
Kerckhoff with a Porsche 1600S,
Jim Fritz with a Cooper Cli-
max, Don Streeter with an AH
Sprite and Paul Streeter with
an MGA.
Included among other early
entries are Carl Dorr of Per-
rine with his Alfa Romeo,
