Riva Uara Midget
Winner at O'Hare
CHICAGO, SEPT. 2-One of
the largest midget auto race
crowds in recent years here to-
night saw Johnny Riva of Des
Plaines, Ill., emerge as victor in
a special United Auto Racing
Association 50-lap Tri-State
Championship test at O'Hare
Stadium.
A full Offy-Ford field of 38
entries time trialed with Riva
setting the fastest time and
starting at the tail end of the
16-car field. It was a torrid
and thrilling grind all the way
with no less than three leaders
and nearly a fourth in Don
Boorse of Milwaukee who was
the night's hard luck king.
Boorse and his powerful Of-
fy were charging right on the
tail of leader Danny Kladis of
Chicago in his own Falcon
midget on the 28th lap when
slower moving Jack Hodge of
Gary, Ind., spun out in his
roadster Offy in the first cor-
ner right in front of Boorse.
Don fought his mount in des-
peration as it whacked the wall
at the top of the turn and fi-
nally careened to a stop going
into the backstretch. He
climbed out unhurt, but his vic-
tory bid was over. Kladis con-
tinued in front, but soon had
to contend with Riva..
Driving the Snelton Offy as
hard as he could, Johnny fi-
nally got the lead on the 41st
lap and then had to withstand
a late charge by veteran Byron
Fisher of Lemont, Ill., in the
Zartler (z) Offy. Riva finally
crossed the finish line with a
three car length win tucked in
his safety belt.
Following Riva and then
Fisher were, in order, Kladis,
point leader Russ Sweedler of
Joliet, Ill., and the leader in
the race for the first four laps,
Billy Wood of Kenosha. Riva's
winning time of 12:43.77 was
slightly off the track record of
12:33.59 set by Fisher's car
owner, Chicagoan Ernie Zart-
ler in 1956.

by Russ Palumbo of Joliet with

Wednesday, September 7, 1960
The Auto Racing Scene
REX WHITE of Spartanburg,
S. C., has made more than his
share of the automobile racing
news within the past week .. .
First off, he's been approached
by a Ford owner, asking him
to switch off the Chevholets
for the 1961 season. He has
reportedly been offered three
cars and a tow truck, but Rex
has not agreed to anything thus
far . . . Next, White bought a
new 1960 Chevrolet last week
and will groom it for the "Na-
tional 400" race at Charlotte
Motor Speedway on Oct. 16. He
also plans to run it in the At-
lanta 500-miler later in the
year. . . . Also, Rex underwent
treatment on an ear last week.
It's been bothering him quite
a bit this season. . . . And fi-
nally, White has advanced his
lead in NASCAR's Grand Na-
tional division national point
standings to 1,830 over second
place Lee Petty of Randleman,
N. C. .. . It's White now with
13,788 points to 11,958 for Pet-
ty. . . . Bobby Johns of Miami,
Fla., is close on Petty's heels
with 11,008 points.
