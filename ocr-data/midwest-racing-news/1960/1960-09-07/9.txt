Wednesday, September 7, 1960
Grand Prix Racing
RIVERSIDE, CALIF.A pair
of compact car races have been
added to the Nov. 20 racing
schedule which features the
second running of the Grand
Prix of the United States at
Riverside, Calif.
The G.P., which is for single-
seat European-type road race
cars, is the concluding event
on the world championship
schedule, to be held this year
on the road course at the Riv-
erside International Raceway.
The compact car races are
divided into two classes, en-
gines 1600 cubic centimeters or
less, and engines over that dis-
placement.
A race for formula junior
cars, smaller versions of the
G.P. machines, is set for the
preceding day (Nov. 19) and
this will count toward the in-
ternational championship in
that division, with the winner
going to the Nassau winter
speed weeks as guest of the
Bahamas Development Board.
Alec Ulmann, who heads an
international aircraft engineer-
ing and parts firm based in
New York, was founder of the
famed Sebring, Fla., sports car
ing ahead of Norm Nelson in a 1960 Ford.
