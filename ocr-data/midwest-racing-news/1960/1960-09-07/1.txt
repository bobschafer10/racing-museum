By Ver-Bet
The "Old Pro," Miles Melius,
found his hometown track at
Slinger filled with Labor Day
weekend racing fans and de-
cided to make a night of it.
Melius started to collect his
laurels from the very begin-
ning of the big night of mod-
fied stock car racing by turn-
ing in fastest qualifying time
(54.87 mph), winning his 10-
lap heat and then adding the
biggest triumph of the evening
—the 100-lap feature for the
20 fastest qualifiers. Melius
put the pistons of his powerful
workhorse to hard use, starting
from the rear of the pack, he
stretched out into the lead on
the 11th lap of the congested
100 lapper and stayed there,
unchallenged to the very vic-
torious finish. Outside of the
normal tire and engine mis-
haps that are common in the
long runs, the only serious in-
cident to appear was on the
84th lap when Bill Palmer's
car, Random Lake, caught fire.
A restart was necessary after
a 10 minute delay-the injury
being suffered by the automo-
bile only. Though Etchie Bier-
tzer, Barton, had 2nd place in
his rear pocket, the 3rd, 4th
and 5th place finishers made
the exciting part of the fea-
ture. How they finished:
Winner, Miles Melius, Sling-
er, 52.63; 2nd, Etchie Biertzer,
Barton; 3rd, Don Schuppel, Ke-
waskum; 4th, Norbie Klemp,
Fredonia; 5th, Ken Tlougan,
Milwaukee.
