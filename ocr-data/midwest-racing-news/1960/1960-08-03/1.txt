USAC Race
to Jeffords
By Robert "Sam" Linde
Jim Jeffords, driving a Cam-
oradi "Birdcage" Maserati, won
his first feature race at Elkhart
Lake's Road America. This
race, the first such USAC pro-
fessional road race at Elkhart
Lake, was not an easy win for
Jeffords. It was not until the
37th lap that Jeffords seemed
to be able to win. At that point,
the leader Augie Pabst, Mil-
waukee, in a Meisterbrauser"
Scarab, was forced to drop out
of the race because of a broken
half shaft in his rear axle. Jef-
fords was then able to drive to
the win. Finishing far behind
Jeffords was Jim Hall of Dallas,
Texas, driving another 2.8
Maserati.
The first leader in this 200
mile event was Carroll Shelby
in the "Old Yaller." This car,
built up of American compon-
ents, is owned and was built by
Max Balchowsky using a Buick
engine. "Old Yaller," with Shel-
by driving, was a combination
that seemed impossible to beat.
Shelby had extended a 36 sec-
ond lead before the transmis-
sion packed up, forcing him to
quit. Pabst, who had been run-
ning second, then took over the
lead until he left the race.
Even though Pabst left the
race, the Scarabs were not com-
pletely beaten. Harry Heuer,
the owner of the two ex-Lance
Reventlow cars, finished a well
driven fourth. Heuer lacks the
experience of Pabst, but the
race Heuer drove was a beauti-
fully controlled one and with-
out mistakes. Third was Loyal
Katskee in another "Birdcage"
Maserati.
Unlike the amateur races, the
professionals have only
two
classes, under and over
two
litres. Finishing first in
the
under two litre class was Bob
Holbert, Warrington, Pa., in a
1600 Porsche RSK. Roger Pen-
ske, who seemed to be the man
to beat in this class, withdrew
because of clutch difficulties.
USAC National Champion, Rod-
ger Ward, was to drive the
Leader Card Sportor, a Porsche
RS. However, he scratched be-
cause of a bent valve. During
the 200 miler, Ward took over
Jack Ensley's Porsche RSK and
drove to a deserved third in

broke the track record and won
