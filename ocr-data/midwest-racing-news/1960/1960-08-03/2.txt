Chilean Benefit Races

and Central American countries
will attend the big Chilean
Benefit races to be held at the
Waukegan Speedway, Friday,
Aug. 5, at 8:30 p.m. The consuls,
all stationed in Chicago, and the
countries they represent are:
J. V. Johansen, Chile; Fritz Ad-

To Etchie Biertzer
Melius Remains in Point Lead
By Jim Engel
CEDARBURG, JULY 27-3,650 persons, the third largest
crowd of the season, saw Edwin "Etchie" Biertzer average 49.42
mph in capturing the 25-lap main event for modified stocks at
the Cedarburg Raceway Wednesday night, July 27. It was his
second main win of the season. Miles Melius, Slinger, who has
been staging a torrid point battle with Biertzer for the 1960
track championship, remains in the point lead with a new total
of 2,115 points.
Biertzer picked up five points
on Melius and now trails by
only 25, with a 2,090 total count.
Melius placed third in the fea-
ture, second in a heat and third
in qualifying runs, earning 195
points towards his standings.
Biertzer's win, plus a fourth
heat win, gave his 175 points
plus a ninth place in qualifying
for 25 additional points, or. a
total of 200 points.
Biertzer took the lead on lap
4 and was out of striking dis-
tance for the remaining 21 laps.
Melius encountered some engine
difficulties while trying for the
number one position, but was
forced to drop back and relin-
quish second spot to Greg
Krieger, Milwaukee, on the 20th

lap.

for the night. Klemp was run-
ning in the top positions when
he blew a left front tire and
spun out. He continued on the

Stock Jalopies
Spin, Crash, Roll
The 15-lap stock jalopy fea-
ture once again provided fans
with countless thrills and spills.
The event was stopped two com-
plete times because of violent
roll overs. The first was by
George Adkins, Milwaukee, who
rolled 3%4 times and the second
was by Roy Siegfried, West Al-
lis, who turned his car over 214
times. Both cars were serious-
ly damaged, and blocked the
track in the middle of the north
turn, necessitating the restarts.
Eight cars out of an original 17
starters finished the full 15 laps.
The evening's purse amount-
