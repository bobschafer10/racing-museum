MIDWEST RACING NEWS
MODIFIEDS AT BEAVER DAM
The management of Beaver
Dam Raceway has scheduled a
special race for Monday night,
August 15, open to all track-
worthy cars for a guaranteed
purse of a thousand fifteen dol-
lars. Purse will be paid on a
basis of 140 points at $7.25 per
qualify in the time trials will
run four ten-lap heats, a twen-
ty-five lap semi and a thirty
lap feature with the consolation
paying four places down, open
to non qualifiers.
This special "Show of Shows"
is open to all comers . . . stock,
modified or what have you.
Drivers must be twenty-one
years of age or older, and cars
must be trackworthy under
rules accepted at Wisconsin
stock car tracks regularly pre-
senting stock and modified
races.
All starts will be inverted
and all cars will be grouped ac-
cording to time trial results on-
ly. In event of scratches in the
heats, race officials will move
up the next fastest qualifiers to
fill out a twenty-car semi and
feature.
This special "Show of Shows"
has been scheduled to permit
fans in the Beaver Dam area to
see the fastest cars and best
drivers this part of the country
can produce. In event of rain,
the special race will be post-
poned until August 22. Top
drivers in all stock and modi-
fied circuits have been enthusi-
astc for the chance to return
to Beaver Dam's fast track and
race fans are eagerly awaiting
the chance to see the forty fast-
est go for a thousand dollars.
Time trils will start at 7:00 p.m.
with the first heat going at 8:30.
