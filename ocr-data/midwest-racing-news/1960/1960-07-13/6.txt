By 1975 U. S. industry will
need almost as much water as
the entire United States uses
now for all purposes combined,
according to Thomas F. Wolfe,
managing director of the Cast
Iron Pipe Research Association,
Chicago.
"Modern industry uses water
in fantastic quantities," Wolfe
continued. "It takes as much as
110,000 gallons of water to pro-
duce one ton of steel. Twenty
gallons of water are required to
refine one gallon of gasoline,
and up to 40,000 gallons of wa-
ter are used to make one ton
of paper."
"Now is the time for citizens
to be concerned with their wa
ter facilities," Wolfe warned,
"not when it becomes a major
problem."
