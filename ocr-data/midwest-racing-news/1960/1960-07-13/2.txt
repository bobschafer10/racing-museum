First Cedarburg Sweep
To Miles Melius
By Jim Engel
Once again it was all Miles Melius at the Cedarburg Race-
way. This time, however, the Slinger ace modified stock car
pilot scored the first clean sweep of the season as he sped to
victory for the third consecutive week in the 25-lap feature
event, set the evening's fastest qualifying time at 51.81 mph,
and won his 10-lap heat race, the fastest of the evening, at 49.56
mph. It was Melius' fifth feature victory in eight starts at Cedar-
burg.
Some 3,650 fans were in at-
tendance and viewed some
mighty fine driving as Melius
powered his way through heavy
traffie to take the lead on lap
nine of the main event and go
on for the win, finishing one
half lap ahead of second place
Billy Johnson of Oconomowoc.
His average speed was clocked
at 49.44 mph.
Fans were not only treated to
good driving, but plenty of hot
action as well. It took three
starts to get the feature under
way. On the first Bob Rataj-
czyk, Kenosha, spun out and
was hit by Ken Tlougan, Mil-
waukee, causing the first re-
start. The second came as Linus
Peters, West Bend, spun out
coming out of the south turn
and was hit by Ted Rozinski,
West Allis, who climbed up on-
to Peters' vehicle and was in
turn hit by Gary Sheldon, Be-
loit, and Eddie Stillman, Mil-
waukee. The accident forced
Rozinski and Sheldon from the
event.
After finally getting the green
flag, the big racing battle took
place between Stillman, Billy
Schwister of Milwaukee, Rataj-
czyk. Etchie Biertzer of Barton,
Johnson and Greg Krieger of
Milwaukee for second place.
The position changed hands sev-
eral times and gave the assem-
blage some real racing thrills.
At the end of 25 laps Johnson
placed second ahead of Biertzer,
Krieger, Stillman, and Rataj-
czyk. Schwister placed eighth
