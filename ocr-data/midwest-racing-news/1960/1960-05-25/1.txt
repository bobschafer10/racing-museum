Record-smashing rookie Jim
Hurtubise of Lennox, Calif.,
blasted the 10-mile qualifying
record at Indianapolis on May
22 with an average speed of
149.056 miles an hour. The rec-
ord was made in the Travelon
Special from the wreck which
caused Ed Elisian's death in a
race in Milwaukee last year.
Hurtubise will be one of four
newcomers to take part in the
final lineup for the 44th 500-
mile auto race May 30 at the
Indianapolis Motor Speedway.
The four laps which Hurtu-
bise ran were faster than any-
one ever thought possible.
The speed of 148 m.p.h. had not
been reached until this dirt
track graduate made his run,
In the three weeks of practice
four cars were wrecked, but no
one was seriously injured. In
last year's practice runs, two
drivers were killed, bringing
the old track's death toll to 50.
The 33 qualifiers averaged a
record of 144.070 miles an hour.
