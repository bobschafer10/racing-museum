MEMORIAL DAY CYCLE EVENT
SET AT CEDARBURG
23rd Annual Race Meet Hosts 70 Entries
On Monday, May 30, the Mil-
−
waukee Motorcycle Club, Inc.
will proudly sponsor the run-
ning of their 23rd Annual
Memorial Day, American Mo-
torcycle Association, sanctioned,
four star, 1/2 mile race meet. It
will be the only professional,
competitive motor event sched-
uled for the Milwaukee area
race fans on this holiday.
The event is again being
made possible thru the cooper-
ation of the Cedarburg Volun-
teer Fire Department, at their
own Firemen's Park, located on
Highway 143, 1/2 mile north of
Highway 57 in Cedarburg. The
firemen will retain 25% of the
gate receipts.
Practice runs begin at 10:00
a.m. on race day, with a field
of some 70 or more entries due
to start qualifying at Noon to
fill the 36 starting positions.
Twelve riders in each class—
Novice, Amateur and Expert-—
will compete in two, three mile
heat events, and must finish in
the top three places in order to
qualify to compete in the finals.
A five mile final, one for each
division will climax the nine
event program.

Gardner Takes
O'Hare Feature
Chicago, May 21-Veteran,
likable Arnie Gardner of St.
Charles, Ill., ended Fred Lor-
enzen's victory string at three
tonight in a crash-filled NAS-
CAR 25-lap main event for late
model stock cars here at O'Hare
Stadium.
Gardner and his '57 Chevro-
let let the event throughout, but
defending track champion Bill
Lutz of Louisville ('57 Chevy)
virtually pushed him across the
finish line. Third was Harry
Simonsen of Chicago in still
another '57 Chevrolet.
Lorenzen, victor in all the
previous late model 25 lappers
here to date, tangled with Jer-
ry Vandermeir of Melrose Park
on the fifth lap. He was able
to get back in the event but
was out of contention. Fred's
closest challenger for the point
lead, Chicagoan Whitey Gerken,
spun out on the backstretch on
lap 12.
The race was action-packed
with no less than eight cars pil-
ing up in the first turn on the
first lap. Eighteeen cars started
the event with 13 still running
at the finish. Winning both the
trophy dash and a heat, Gerken
was the night's only double
winner.
