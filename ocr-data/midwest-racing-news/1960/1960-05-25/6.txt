=== COLUMN 4 ===
One of the top dragsters this year appears to
be "Tuffy I," which is owned and driven by Pete
Farnsworth of Milwaukee. Pete recently at Union
Grove, Wis., turned a 9.58 e.t. and a speed of 160.71
m.p.h., as he was the day's top eliminator. Shown in
photo are (left to right) Pete Farnsworth, Mrs. L.
Farnsworth, and mechanic Bill Adam of Evanston,
Illinois. The dragster is powered by a 1951 Chrysler
using eight carburetors to supply it with its needed
fuel, which is "nitro." Pete's car is exceptionally
well-built, stressing many fine safety features.
Stocks to Roar
Again Next Sunday!
The third program of modi-
fied stock car racing at the
Waukegan Speedway is set to
roll next Sunday night, May 29,
on the big Memorial Day week-
end. Action should be swift as
the powerful cars battle for po-
sition on the fast quarter-mile
surface. Time trials get under
way at 7:15, races at 8:30. Track
manager Mike Kaishian has
promised fans another action
packed amateur event. The
speedway is located on West
Washington Street bewteen the
Green Bay road and the Skokie
Highway.
