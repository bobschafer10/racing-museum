MIDWEST RACING NEWS
Special
TOP: Troy Ruttmann of Dearborn, Mich., in the John Zink
Heater Spl., will start in the second row outside. His qualifying
speed was 145.366. The car was built by A. J. Watson and will
have D. Moore as head mechanic. Ruttmann is an ex-Indy winner.
BOTTOM: Jimmy Bryan of Phoenix, Arizona, in the Metal-
Cal. Spl. As the photo shows this car is of a new design with a
very narrow nose. The car is entered by George Salih, who is
also the builder and chief mechanic. Bryan is also a previous
Indy winner. His starting position will be the inside of the fourth
row. Qualifying speed for Jimmy Bryan was 144.532.
