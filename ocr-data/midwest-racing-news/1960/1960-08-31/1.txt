Record Purse
$35,600
By Al Krause
MILWAUKEE, WIS., Aug.
28-Hard luck for Tony Bet-
tenhausen was good luck for
Len Sutton in the 200 mile na-
tional championship speedway
big car race here on the one
mile paved track today. Betten-
hausen led from the 60th mile
until his Dowgard Special
caught fire on the 182nd mile
with Tony riding the car to
the pits.
A crowd of 26,156 that con-
tributed to a record-smashing
purse of $35,600 saw the slim
Portland, Ore. driver drop into
seventh spot at the start of the
race in Pete Salemi's light
brown No. 9 and then stay near
the leaders for the rest of the
way. Sutton also picked off the
beautiful Leader Car trophy
put up by Bob Wilke. Wilke
also presented his driver, Rod-
ger Ward with the fastest qual-
ifying trophy. Ward qualified
at 34.83 seconds (102.9 miles
per hour).
The race could be called the
"Heartbreak 200" for Tearing
Tony. The Dutchman, winner
of two races here earlier in the
week in the midgets and stock
cars, was the whole show until
he dropped out. Tony estab-
lished new records for 75, 100,
125, 150, and 175 miles. Sutton,
also in a record breaking mood,
broke the 200 mile mark, his
time was 1 hour, 59 minutes,
50.06 seconds (100.14 miles per
hour). After the race Sutton
stated that he had less than a
gallon of gasoline in the car.
Victory might have gone to
Parnelli Jones, the talented
Torrance, Calif speedster. Jones
tailed Bettenhausen, lost the
second spot to A. J. Foyt, and
then saw Foyt take the lead
when Tony went out. Foyt was
then forced to the pits for fuel,
Jones took the lead but it was
short-lived. He too had to go to
the pits as he couldn't get the
reserve tank to function. That
dropped Jones back to 13th
spot. Sutton, then got into the
lead and nursed it the rest of
the way.
