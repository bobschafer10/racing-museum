Sign of the Times
Look for the nation's four
big major stock car speedways,
Charlotte, Atlanta, Daytona,
and Darlington-to push their
"big league" theme. Each of
the four now has two long-dis-
tance races annually, total prize
money for the 3,350 miles of
racing to top the half-million
dollar make. Charlotte leads
with 1,000 miles for the "World
600" and "National 400." At-
lanta has its "Dixie 300" and a
500-miler. Darlington has its
"Rebel 300" and "Southern
500", and Daytona has a 500
and the "Firecracker 250".
Charlotte also heads the prize
money list with some $160,000,
the "World 600" hitting $107,-
775 and the upcoming "Nation-
al 400" on Oct. 16 with more
than $50,000. Officials of the
Charlotte track also report that
both ticket orders and requests
for information are running
well ahead of "World 600" in-
terest, an indication, they say,
that fans are seeking to avoid
a last-minute rush similar to
that of the "World 600" when
ticket offices had to be kept
open all night before the race
to serve the influx of fans.
