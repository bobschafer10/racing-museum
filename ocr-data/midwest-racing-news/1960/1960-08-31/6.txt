By Jim Engel
In an action packed program
of modified and stock jalopy
racing, Gary Sheldon, a young
Beloit, Wis., speedster, came
home the victor in the main
event, a thrill-packed 25-lap
feature for the modifieds. The
win was Sheldon's very first
on a dirt track. He has won
previously on asphalt surfaces.
His winning speed was 49.13
m.p.h.
Over 2,800 fans witnessed the
evening's action which proved
to be one of the most thrilling
evenings at the track. Five
roll-overs highlighted the thrills
along with an uncountable
number of spins and crashes.
Greg Krieger of Milwaukee,
the top point leader at the
track, was sidelined for the
evening with his own car, but
managed to pick up a ride in
the car usually driven by last
year's champion, Eddie Still-
man, another Milwaukeean.
Krieger's car broke a pinion
gear in Saturday night racing
at Hales Corners. He placed
fifth in qualifying and took
third place in the main event.

=== COLUMN 3 ===
MIDWEST RACING NEWS
Only half of the field managed
a finish in the 14 car, ten lap
heat, and only nine completed
the 15 lap feature out of an
original starting field of 26
cars. Ed Smith of Waukegan
flipped his Plymouth coupe
three complete times on the
first lap, and a lap later, Way-
mon Jackson of Wauconda
flipped over in his Hudson.
Eddie Jones, a favorite in the
amateur contests, emerged vic-
torious in both events.
The back up race provided
fans with additional thrills as
the stocks took one lap around
the track in reverse gear, then
turned around, and completed
another lap in reverse gear,
running in the opposite direc-
tion. Results showed several
demolished cars. Blaine Leath-
ermann, Waukegon, won the
event, but just as he crossed
the line, completing his second
lap, he was hit by another
comer who was completing his
first, and the impact sent him
flipping over on his side.
Drivers competed for $1,350
in prize monies.
Next Sunday, Sept. 4, the
Speedway will enter its final
month of racing for the 1960
season. It will be the 16th Sun-
day night program and track
manager Mike Kaishian will
stage another series of stock
jalopy racing along with the
running of the modifieds. It
will be Jewel Tea Employee
night at the track with the
butchers of the chain food
stores competing in a special
added race of their own. Time
trials will start at 7:15 with
racing getting under way at
8:30.
