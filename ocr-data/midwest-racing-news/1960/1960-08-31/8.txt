Nick Lupo, Milwaukee, Wis.,
made his debut with his new
B open gas dragster, a highly
successful one last Sunday,
Aug. 28, by copping both "top
speed" and "top eliminator"
honors at the Great Lakes
Dragaways weekly program.
He turned a very nifty 134.32
m.p.h. with a low elapsed time
of 10.63 seconds. This new
dragster is a sharp looking ma-
chine, being designed and built
for appearance as well as speed.
Judging from the performance
of Sunday's running, Lupo has
been successful on both counts.
Drag racing fans should get
many thrills from this new ad-
dition to the dragster field.

Rookie of the Year
It looks at the moment like
NASCAR'S "Rookie of the
Year" crown is still very much
up for grabs, what with no one
like last year's winner, Richard
Petty of Randleman, N.C., set-
ting a sizzling pace to claim
the honor . . . If the award goes
to a Grand National Division
driver, it'll probably be one of
four-Ned Jarrett of Newton,
N.C., Gerald Duke of College
Park, Ga., David Pearson of
Spartanburg, S.C. or Doug
Yates of Chapel Hill, N.C.-
All four are in their first full
season in the late model ranks,
yet neither is technically a full-
fledged rookie. All have rather
extensive racing experience
under their belts. Jarrett is 6th
in national point standings
while Duke is 13th, Pearson
18th and Yates 20th. Any of
NASCAR'S other divisions,
meanwhile, could come up with
a young sensation.
