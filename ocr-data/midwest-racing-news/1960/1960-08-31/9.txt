Saturday night at the Ken-

weeks of racing for the "Big
Dane," Norm Nelson of Ra-
cine, Wisconsin. Two Satur-

stock car feature win at Wil-
mot (in fact, a clean sweep),
then on the next day, Sunday,
a 4th place finish in the Cham-
pionship Late Model Stock Car
Races at Terra Haute, Ind. This
was followed by a 3rd place
Modified Stock Feature at Wil-
mot one week ago Saturday,
followed up by the Sunday tri-
umph in winning the 150 mile
National Championship Late
Model Stock Car race at the
State Fair grounds in Milwau-
kee, Wis. This important con-
test of speed put Nelson well
in the lead in National stand-
ings and on Thursday, Nelson
collected more of those valu-
able points by finishing 2nd in
the 200 mile Fair Week spec-
tacular in Milwaukee.
Nelson's winning ways con-
tinued when this past Saturday
night at Wilmot he posted fast-
est qualifying honors (52.20
m.p.h.) and won the 25 lap
modified feature (51.42 m.p.h.)
and appears certain of winning
the 1960 Chevrolet El Camino
which is being awarded to top
man in points at the Wilmot
Speedway.
Only extreme misfortunes on
the forthcoming last two Sat-
urday nights of racing could
eliminate Norm from the cov-
eted top position with Bob
Karry, Lake Villa, Ill., ready
to move up in the event of
such occurrences. Bill Shoul-

Beloit, Wis. Jack Radtke, Grays

Johnson, Czach
At O'Hare
CHICAGO, Aug. 28 - The
flying Swede from Highland
Park, Ill., Erik Johnson, and
Chicago's Roy Czach, new king
of the NASCAR Midwest
Championship division, racked
up new triumphs in NASCAR
late model stock car main
events here at O'Hare Stadium
Friday and Saturday nights.
Johnson and his 1957 Mar-
tinelli Bros. Chevrolet estab-
lished a clean sweep perform-
ance for the night Friday by
taking the checker in the fast
car trophy dash, a 10-lap heat
race and finally the main event.

season.
