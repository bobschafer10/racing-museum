in the three late model national championship stock car races
That one thing is that Tony Bettenhausen, Tinley Park, Ill.,
knows the track better than the rest of the drivers. Betten-
hausen furnished ample proof that he does as he won the 200
mile late model race here today before a crowd of nearly 12,000.

Melius Again Sweeps
Beaver Dam Program
For the second time in as
many tries, Miles Melius, ace
modified stock car pilot out of
Slinger, Wis., scored another
clean sweep of events, as the
Beaver Dam Raceway staged
its second modified "go" of the
season Monday night, Aug. 29.
A crowd of 2,100 persons paid
to a purse of $1,150 and wit-
nessed the 10 event program
which boasted 135 laps of com-
petitive racing.
In the 30-lap main event,
Melius needed only five laps
to take over the number one
spot from the rear of the field
and breeze on to victory, fin-
ishing ½th lap ahead of Greg
Krieger of Milwaukee. Melius
also posted the night's fastest
qualifying time when he turned
the quarter-mile clay surface
oval at 49.95 m.p.h. He also
took top honors in the fourth
heat event.
