Season's Largest Crowd Turns Out
Before the largest crowd of the current season, 3,250, Bob
Ratajczyk of Kenosha, turned the tables on Greg Krieger of
Milwaukee and prevented the red-hot Milwaukee pilot from
scoring his third consecutive clean sweep and his fourth con-
secutive feature victory. Ratajczyk took full advantage of a
front row position in the fast eighteen car field and went on for
packed finishes in local area
stock car racing when Gordy
Gorman, Libertyville, breezed
home to victory averaging 46.03
mph, in the 15 lap semi feature
contest. Only 12 cars of an orig-
inal 24 starters were able to
finish. The most spectacular ac-
cident occurred in the 14th and
15th laps of the race when Buzz
Gleason, Milwaukee, spun side-
ways on the main stretch and
was clobbered by Ken Becker
of Lake Villa. The front end of
Gleason's machine was nearly
wiped out and on the 15th lap,
Fran Seamars, Milwaukee,
tapped the stalled cars and went
into the wall where his engine
burst into flames. While fans
had their eyes glued on these
series of events, Gorman snuck
past leader Glen Eichmann of
Lake Zurich, who had led the
contest throughout the event,
but was slowed by the mishaps.
The fire was quickly extin-
guished by track officials and
as Seamars was being pushed
into the pit area, the car again
caught fire and became another
blazing inferno. Again the blaze
was quenched and both times
Seamars did not suffer any
burns, thanks to the strictly en-
forced safety rules requiring full
firewalls and floorboards in all
racing automobiles.
