Photo shows the "Orange Crate", so named be-
cause of its beautiful orange finish. Lorenz Patryn
and Ray Piasecki are the owner and driver, both
of Fox Lake, Ill. Bob Dixon of Milwaukee is the
mechanic. The dragster is powered by 283 cubic
inch Chevrolet engine, Clay-Smith cam, and a 1957
Chevrolet fuel injection system. The body is a
1950 Crosley.
Top speed turned to date is 12.36 et at 115 mph.
"The Orange Crate" is top in the B/Altered Class.
The last two out of three weeks the dragster was
top eliminator and took top speed honors at Union
Grove. Three carburetors supply the fuel, which
is gas.
