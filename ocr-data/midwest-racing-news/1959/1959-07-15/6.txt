MILES MELIUS SETS FAST TIME
WINS FEATURE EVENT
Newcomer to the Waukegan
Speedway, Miles Melius
Slinger, Wisconsin took nearly
all honors as modified stock
car racing went into its ninth
week Sunday night, July 12th.
Although Melius is new to
Waukegan, making his first
appearance since its reopening
three years ago, -Melius is by
no means a newcomer to stock
car racing. He has won several
track championships in his 11
years of racing and currently
is the point leader at Hales
Corners and Cedarburg race
tracks in Wisconsin.

MELIUS LIKES
WAUKEGAN TRACK
After winning the 25 lap
feature race at the Waukegan
Speedway, Sunday night, July
12, Miles Melius praised the
track's condition claiming it to
be one of the smoothest racing!
surfaces he has ever driven on.
In addition to smoothness, it
was well watered, making it
tacky enough to enable drivers
to get a good bite with a lot
of traction. Melius plans to re-
turn and try for two in a row
at Waukegan on Sunday nite,
July 19th.
