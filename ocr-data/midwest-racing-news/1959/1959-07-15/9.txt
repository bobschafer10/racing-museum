DAYTONA BEACH
NEWS
In the two major stock car
races staged over the new and
high banked Daytona Interna-
tional Speedway, the NASCAR
late model speedsters have
covered a total distance of 26,-
739 miles without a single cau-
tion flag to establish two world
record speeds for stock cars.
In the "NASCAR Interna-
tional Sweepstakes" last Feb-
ruary, 59 drivers competed in
the 500 mile race with Lee Pet-
ty of Randleman, N. C., win-
ning with a record 500 mile
speed of 135.521 miles per hour
in a 1959 Oldsmobile. In this
race, the 53 drivers covered a
total distance of 19,157 miles
in the total of 7,582 laps of
race coverage.
In the NASCAR "Firecrack-
er 250" late model sweepstakes
race staged July 4, 37 drivers
covered a total of 3,33 laps or
7,582 miles over the two gand a
half mile course. with Glenn
"Fireball" Roberts of Daytona
Beach averaging 140.58 miles
per hour in a 1959 Pontiac.
This beat the Indianapolis 250
mile speedway car record of
138.039 miles per hour set this
year by Rodger Ward. Both
races were staged without  a
single caution flag marring the
performances.
In addition to these two long
distance races, the NASCAR
late model stock cars also com-
pleted two 100 mile races over
the same high speed course
without encountering any prob-
lems to slow down the pace.
