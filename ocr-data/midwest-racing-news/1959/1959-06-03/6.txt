RUSS MEYER SPECIAL
LEADING IN POINT
STANDINGS
Eddie Stillman of Milwau-
kee is rapidly becoming the
nimber 1 driver in the Milwau-
kee area circuit. He is the
only driver who so far this
season has won four featuro
race events. He has the dis-
tinction of winning the open-
ing night events at all three
tracks operated by Midwest
May. 9, the opener at Wauke-
gan on May 10, and the opener
at Cedarburg on May 27.
Stillman duplicated his Wau-
kegan win by placing first
and setting a new 25-lap track
record at the
.. Waukegan
May 24. (This record has
since been broken by Milwau-
kee's Bob Ratajezyk). Still-
man, who placed third in the
Milwaukee stock car racing
Association's point standings
in 1958, is trying hard for
the number 1 position. The
two men who placed above
him in that system, Miles Mel-
ius of Slinger and Ken Lou-
gan of Milwaukee, have not
fared' so well thus far this
year. Melius won his first and
only feature event at the
Slinger Speedway last Sun-
day night. TLougan has yet
to finish among the top three
positions. Stillman was the
Waukegan Speedway Track
Champion in 1958 and is well
on his way towards gaining
the 1959 Championship there.
Stillman drives a Model A
Ford powered  by a. 1958
Thunderbird engine.
