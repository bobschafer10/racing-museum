Dentici Is
Victor At
Fair Park
The 3rd Quarter Midget
Race of the 1959 season got
under way Sunday, May 31.
Time trials started at 1:30 p.m.
with the usual amount of mo-
tor trouble. Jim Dentici, who
won the 20-lap feature, also
had the fast time of 9.17. Af-
ter "The Star Spangled Ban-
ner" was sung, starter Clyde
Ehlers presented Dentici nis
trophy for having the fastest
time in time trials.
The first of the 10-lap heat
races then followed. As the
cars jockeyed for position, Bil-
ly Rohn and Gary Lushene
fought it out for the lead. In
the third lap, Lushene spun
his car, andRohn drove on to
win.
The second heat was run off
without mishap. Roger Moore
emerged the victor.
The third heat got underway
smoothly enough, but didn't
end that way. Kay. Anthenson
emerged the victor. The race
featured a spin-out which saw
four cars disqualified.
Billy Chandler won the
fourth heat of the afternoon.
This heat demanded a restart
as Ken Boyce's car developed
engine trouble and stopped.
The novice events then fol-
lowed. The races were as ex-
citing as ever. Chuck Stanelle
West Allis rookie, won two of
the three events. Stanelle won
his heat as well as the feature.
Terry Moore won the other no
vice heat.
Kay Anthenson (women's
world's answer to Roger
Ward) won the first sem-
feature of the afternoon. Miss
Anthenson started in the first
row outside, and lead the en-
tire race.
Roger Moore again showed
his expert driving ability as he
won the second semi-feature,
his second victory of the after-
noon.
The consolation race deman-
ded a restart as Ray Ehlers
spun his machine in the first
lap.
The clas B feature was won
by Ken Boyce in a duel with
two other cars.
The feature event of the af-
ternoon was won by Jim Den-
tici. Dentici, showing a fine
array of driving techniques,
lapped the first five cars on
his way to victory.
By Bob Key
