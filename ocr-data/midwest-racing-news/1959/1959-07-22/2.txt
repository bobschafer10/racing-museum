day at Williams Grove Speed-
way. The car was the one in

injured three months ago.
Johnson, a seven year vet-

pion in 1957 and 1958.
The fatal accident occurred
when Joe Barzda tangled with
Johnson, causing both cars to
overturn. Barzda escaped with
minor injuries.
The race was halted after
