ELEVENTH PROGRAM
SET AT WAUKEGAN
POWDER PUFF DERBY TO HIGHLIGHT
EVENING'S EVENTS
Modified stock car racing
moves into its eleventh big
week at the Waukegan Speed-
way' on Sunday night, July 26.
Track manager Michael Kaish-
ian is scheduling another Pow-
der Puff Race as an added at-
traction. The Powder Puff is
made up of women drivers rac-
ing against one another for 10
trips around the track. They
will be driving both modifieds
and strictly stock machines,
anything they can get their
hands on. Anything can happen
- and usually does  as the
women try their skill at the
racing game.
Heading the field of nearly
60 cars. and drivers will be
point leader' Eddie Stillman of
Milwaukee, who will again
have his car in working order
for . Sunday's race. Stillman
has been the only driver to win
three feature events at the
Speedway thus far this year
and is leading the field of 84
drivers who have shared in the
point standings with a total of
1860 points.
Miles Melius of Slinger, Wis-
consin, who will also be on
hand, will be giving Stillman
some rough competition as he
tries for his third consecutive
feature win. Melius, who has
made two appearances, has
qualified the fastest on both
occasions, won the feature on
both occasions, and placed third
