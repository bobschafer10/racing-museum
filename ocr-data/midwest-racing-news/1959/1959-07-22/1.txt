Leads From First
Lap to Finish
Sots 3 New Records
By Al Krause
MILWAUKEE, WIS., July
19 - When Tony Bettenhausen
wins an auto race, he wins it
in convincing fashion, Today
the Tinley Park Dutehman
shot from his number three
spot into the lead on the first
lap and went on to lead the
150 mile late model stock car
race the rest of the way by
varying margins before a
crowd of 21,555. Bettenhausen
drove his No. 99 1958 Ford to
three new records to claim the
big end of the $16,300 purse
and the huge trophy put up by
N. Perry Luster and his Nation-
al Racing Affiliates.
This was the seventh nation-
al championship racing win
for Tearing Tony since 1950.
He's won two each in midgets
and speedway big cars. His last
stock car win was in August,
1955 when he won a '150 miler
during state fair week. Betten-
hausen was driving the 1958
Ford that Rodger Ward bought
from Jerry Unser's family ear-
lier this month.
