As Records Fall
L" Eddie Stillman of Mil-
waukee set a new track record
in winning the 25-lap feature
race at the Cedarburg Raceway
this Wednesday night. Still-
man's speed of 49.42 m.p.h. e-
rased the old record of 49.35,
whieh Stillman also held as of
the 27th of May of this year.
Stillman started near the rear
of the 18-car field and moved
through the heavy and fast
traffic to take over the lead
position from Len Kannie of
Kenosha on the 9th lap. Miles
Melius of Slinger, who manag-
ed to move from 18th position
to 8th position in four laps,
was forced to drop out of the
race because of a burned out
transmission. By the 15th lap
Stillman took a commanding
lead of about 1/8 lap and never
was again challenged. Etchie
Biertzer of Barton moved into
second spot on the 17th lap,
and from here on in it was a
four-way battle for third place
between Kannie, Billy Johnson
of Oconomowoc, Carl Kulow of
Plymouth, and Don Schuppel
of Kewaskum. Stillman's win
makes his second at Cedarburg
and his seventh of the season
at all tracks. In winning he
broke Miles Melius' two-win
streak. Biertzer finished se-
cond, Johnson, third, Kulow
was fourth, Schuppel fifth, and
Kannie placed sixth.
The 15-lap semi-feature was
won by Doug Bandl of Milwau-
kee at a speed of 47.03 m.p.h.
as Ted Rozinski of Milwaukee
placed second, and Homer An-
sell placed third.
