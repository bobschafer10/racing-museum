Daytona Beach
SCCA Races
DAYTONA BEACH, FLA.,
Aug. - Forty-five drivers,
headed by Joe Sheppard of
Tampa and other outstanding
Florida stars, have filed en-
tries for the Florida Region
car races sched-
SCCA sports
uled for the 3.81 mile sports
car course at Daytona Interna-
tional Speedway for Saturday
and Sunday, September 5 and 6.
The two-day program with
practice and qualifying trials
on Saturday and three races on
Sunday is being presented un-
der the supervision of the Cen-
tral Florida Region of the
Sports Car Club of America
and will mark the first compe-
tition for the SCCA group over
the new three million dollar.
Daytona track.
The sports car course
em-
braces the two and a half mile
high banked track with it's 31
degree elevated turns plus an
asphalt, infield road that com-
bines to make a 3.81 mile
course, something entirely dif-
ferent from any other sports
car course in the country and
the only one of comparative
size completely visible to spec-
tators.
The Sunday program opens
with a 15-lap, 47-mile race for
E production and above and F
modified and above cars, fol-
lowed by another 15-lap race
for smaller cars at 2 o'clock
and then the 150-mile feature
at 3:30 p.m. which will be
limited to 75 starting cars.
