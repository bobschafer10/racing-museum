Don Klug Critically Burned
In one of the most  serious
accidents of all times in the lo-
cal area, Don Klug, 28, of
Saukville was critically burned
when his modified stock car
tangled with other cars, rolled
over four to five times, and
burst into flames. The accident
happened going into the north
turn on the first lap of the
first 10-lap heat event of the
evening. Klug's machine burst
into flames on the third roll
and landed upright. It took
track officials approximately 45
to 60 seconds to extinguish the
blaze and remove Klug from
the machine. He was rushed to
St. Alphonsus Hospital in Port
Washington, treated, and sent
to the new burn center at St.
