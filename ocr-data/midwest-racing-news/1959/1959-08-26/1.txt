AT FAIR
Lorenzen
Ward Duel
FOUR ACCIDENTS
By Al Krause
Milwaukee, Wis., Aug. 23 -
Rodger Ward of Indianapolis
continues to have the King
Midas touch.
The 1959 500 mile race win-
ner moved into the lead on the
124th mile and went on to vic
tory in the 150 mile national
championship late model stock
car race at state fair park. The
race is the kickoff of the sixth
annual
speed-o-rama during
fair week. Ward averaged
82.815 miles per hour to win
the rain-riddled race that was
witnessed by a crowd of 16,098.
Fred Lorenzen, Elmhurst, IlI.
picked off the Perry Luster sil-
ver tray as the fastest quali-
fier in his 1958 Ford with a
time of 40.16 seconds (87.649
miles per hour.) Gene Marmor,
Chicago, tied Lorenzen as the
fastest qualifier by Fast Fred-
dy took his time earlier. Ward
then came on to pick up the
Perry Luster trophy as the
winner.
Four separate accidents saw
the yellow light go on for 27
miles while another nine miles
werelost getting the race un
der way as the cars circled' the
track helping to dryit out.
Iggy Katona spun his 1958
Ford in the south turn on the
58th mile and was hit by Bill
Shoulders and Jack Holbrook.
