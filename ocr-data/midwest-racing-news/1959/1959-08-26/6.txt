ROCKFORD

Bill
Horstmeyer of Madison gave
Billy Wood of Kenosha Some
real stiff competition at the

came in second in the 50-lap
feature event leading the race
for 45-laps. Wood was the vic-
tor in the feature race.

fered severe lacerations of the
face, right elbow and loss of
teeth when his car and one dri-

headlong into the outside re-
taining wall. Goudie escaped
without injury, but both cars
were badly damaged. Jones
was the fastest qualifier of the
night and was moving up fast
through the pack from his last
row starting position when the
accident occured.
The only other winner of the
evening was Owen Snyder of
Janesville. Owen tooled his
tive Special out in front of the
pack after two laps and was
never challenged by any of the
other cars.
