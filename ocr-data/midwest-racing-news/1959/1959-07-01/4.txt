If any driver on the modified
stock car circuit in southeas-
tern Wisconsin can be regard-
ed as the Rex Mays of his
group it would have to be Carl
Kulow of Plymouth, Wis. Now
in his late 30's, Kulow has been
in stock car racing since 1950.
Along with his younger broth-
er, Hal, they represent one of
the better brother driving com-
binations active in racing to-
day in this area.
A cheese taster by trade,
Carl was in the army four
years in World War II, win-
ning the combat infantryman's
badge and five battle stars in
the 164th Combat Engineers.
His combat record began on
the beaches of Normandy and
wound up in southeastern Ger-
many.
In 1953 when the Milwaukee
Stock Car Racing Association
agreed to furnish the cars and
drivers for fair park events,
Carl was the winner of the o-
pening program's feature race.
