AT O'HARE
By Bud Booth
Chicago, June 27-Although
competing against the toughest
opposition to date this year,
"Bullet Bill" Lutz of Louisville,
Ky., tonight won his fourth
straight 25-lap New-Late Mo-
del stock car main event of the
season in the fast time of
6:45.23. He wheels Tom's Stan-
dard Service 1957 Chevrolet.
Trying hard, but simply un-
able to catch the flying Lutz
was two-time champion Gene
Marmor of Schiller Park, Il..
('57 Chevrolet) who held the
lead for a short two laps early
in the race. He took over from
the early pacesetter, Red Swan-
berg of Meadowdale, Ill., ('56
Chevrolet)on lap 7.
Lutz then came flying
through from far back in the
field and gradually moved away
to win by seven car lengths.
Following runnerup Marmor
was Fred Lorensen of Elm-
hurst ('56 Chevrolet), last
years O'Hare and National
stock car champion, who did a
tremendous job in holding off
fourth placer Bud Koehler of
Blue Island, Ill. ('58 Studeba-
ker.)
Koehler, making one of his
rare O'Hare appearances, cap-
tured the second heat race for
the New-Late Models. The
other went to Butch Linden of
Deerfield, Ill. ('56 Chevrolet).

fastest qualifiers was captured
