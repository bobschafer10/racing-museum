KEN TLOUGAN SCORES FIRST|
Driving like the champion he is, with his car
functioning as a champion's should, Ken Tlougan of
Milwwaukee won the 25-lap feature for modified
stocks at the Hales Corners Speedway Saturday
night, August 1. It marks Tlougan's first Hales Cor-
ners win for the 1959 season. Tlougan, who was
track champion at Hales Corners in 1957 and again
in 1958, moved through the fast field to take the
lead on the 4th lap. At the drop of the green flag,
Tlougan moved from 14th in the field to fourth spot
on the first turn, driving hard on the outside and
passing the slower cars. By the second lap he had
moved into second position, and two laps later, took
the lead. By the 10th lap, he had more than a half
lap lead on second place Miles Melius and went on
to win the event with an average speed of 54.89 mph.

averaged 50.43 M.P.H. to win
