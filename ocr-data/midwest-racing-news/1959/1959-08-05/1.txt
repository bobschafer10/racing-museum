RAIN HALTS FEATURE
AFTER 16 LAPS
By Tom Bloom
SUN PRAIRIE, WiS.: Billy
Wood of Kenosha increased his
prestige and still remains the
man to beat this year as he
copped three races again Sun-
day night at the Angell Park
Speedway in Sun Prairie. The
talented young driver from
Kenosha came through in fine
style in winning two 10-lap
events and the 25-lap feature
race of the evening.
As usual the time trials got
underway promptly at 7 p.m.
and Tony Lenti of Chicago led
the qualifiers with a sizzling
17.85 on the lightening fast 1½3
mile high banked track. Right
behind Lenti were two Kenosha
veterans, Mike Ladd and Billy
Wood with 18.01 and 18.02.
Rounding out the six fastest
qualifiers were Bill Horstmey-
er and Bob Clements of Madi-
son and Owen Snyder of Janes-
ville.
The first heat race of the
evening saw three of the seven
starters forced out due to
mechanical trouble. The beauti-
ful No. 50 Fredrickson roadster
driven by Barry Congdon of
Whitewater was the first car
to drop out of competition and
he was followed by Jack An-
derson of Racine and John
Hancock of Kenosha. Ander-
son suffered burns on his legs
when his engine blew up and
sprayed him with hot oil. The
event was won by Gene Russo
who is a member of the famous
Russo racing family of Keno-
sha, Wisconsin. Russo took on
ea ly lead and had little trou-
ble in winning the race' after
three cars dropped out. Dick
