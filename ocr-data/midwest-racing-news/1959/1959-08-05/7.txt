Lutz Takes 13th Feature
At O'Hare Stadium
By Bud Booth
He once again received his
stiffest challenge from defend-
ing champ Fred Lorenzen.
Fred, in fact, was leading the
race until Leroy Roberts of
Elmwood Park, IIl. ('57 Chev-
rolet), blew his engine directly
in front of Lorenzen and the
advancing Lutz.
Both Lorenzen and Lutz
piled heavily into the east turn
wall, but Bill was lucky enough
to suffer less damage and was
immediately underway without
losing much time. Lorenzen
lost close to a lap and finished
out of the running.
Lutz, who scored a clean
sweep, was followed across the
finish line by Arnie Gardner of
Geneva, Ill. ('57 Chevrolet),
and Bryant Tucker of Hillside,
IIl. ('57 Buick), in second and
thrid spots respectively.
