Modified stock car racing | spot in the over-all point stand-
moves into its fourteenth week
-
at the Waukegan Speedway
Sunday night, August 16. High-
lighting the program will be
three added attractions consist-
ing of spectator racing, a
thrill-packed roll-over contest,
and a demolition derby.
Track manager Mike Kai-
shian announced that the roll-
over contest will feature strict-
ly stock automobiles being
deliberately rolled over and
over until unable to run. Points
are scored on the basis of the
number of times each driver
is able 'to turn his machine
over. He gains one point if he
tips on his side, two if he lands
on his roof, three for a three-
quarter flip, and four if he
goes over and back on his
wheels again. All cars still able
to run, plus those failing to
turn over in the contest will be
demolished in the demolition
derby.
Miles Melius remains the
man to beat as the powerful
modifieds put on their usual
program of four 10-lap heats,
a 15-lap semi final, and a 25-
lap main event. Melius, in
only five appearances, . has
racked up 1315 points. He still
remains in seventh place, but
this week has a very good
chance of moving into fifth Highway.
