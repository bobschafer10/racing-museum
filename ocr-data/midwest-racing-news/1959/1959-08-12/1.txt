By Bud Booth
Chicago, Aug. 8 - Bill Lutz
of Louisville, Ky., moved a
step closer to the 1959 STAR
driving championship here at
O'Hare Stadium as he racked
up his 14th feature race win of
the year in the 1957 Tom's
Standard Service Chevrolet.
Slowed somewhat by an en-
gine that acted up briefly dur-
ing warmups, Lutz gave little
evidence of any problems once
the competitive action began
and moved out to win the
trophy dash and the fast heat
for the late models before the
main event.

petition once again came from
defending O'Hare and National
