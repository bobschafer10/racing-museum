Kladis Victor at Joliet
JOLIET, ILL., June 6th
For the first time that any-
one can remember, three driv-
ers tied for fast time in quali-
fications. The fast time of
19.42 sec., was'tuned in by No.
57 Bob Tattersall, No. 38 Geo.
Sellery and No. 27 wild Willie
Wildhaber.
The first heat was restarted
due to a spin out. The restart
saw Newt White forgetting to
shut off going, into the num-
ber three corner and hurtled
the guard railing, flipping in
the air. Newt received only a
slight rib injury.
The first heat also had No.
84 Art Scederberg and No. 35
Jim Hurtubise spin out. Danny
Kladis was the eventual winner.
In the second heat No. 58 Bill
Knight lost a wheel and Pat
Dellegrazie and Bob Tomlinson
spun out. Wilhelmie in No. 55
was the victor.
The third heat had spin outs
by No. 9 Phil Ashline, No. 59
Bob Lithgow and No. 53 Paul
Kerber. The victor emerged No.
77 Russ Sweedler.
The fourth heat was won by
No. 50 John Riva after No. 58
Bill Knight and No. 18 Bob
Tomlinson spun out.
In the trophy dash No. 35
Jim Hurtubise left the track
and the race was restarted.
Mike King drove in this race,
the first time since being hurt
last summer. Bob Tattersall
emerged the victor.
The Semi was very fast and
was won by Bob Tattersall.

Electric Fuel Pumps Ruled
Acceptable for IMCA Stock
Cars
An increasing number of re-
quests from IMCA officials,
stock car drivers and owners
for the allowance of electric
fuel pumps has been favorably
acted upon by Association Sec-
retary, John Libby.
Mr. Libby stated, "Mechani
cal fuel pumps are proving
more and more unreliable for
use on the half mile tracks of
our circuit. In, the best inter-
ests of all concerned we have
decided to allow the use of
electrically operated models
for the remainder of 1959.
