Curcio Season
Champ at Wilmot
By Mike Dunn
WILMOT, WIS. - 2,733 peo-
ple turned out for a beautiful
night of stock car racing. Milt
Curcio, proceded to turn in the
fastest time of the evening,
50.14 mph. Although this was
not a exceptionally fast time,
it was fast enough to beat out
the efforts of such drivers as
Erik Johnson, Eddie Stillman,
Karl Kulow, and Bobby Rata-
jezyk.
Bob Lerick, of Great Lakes,
Ill. got the show off to a slam
bang start as he lost control
of his car in the third lap of
the first heat. Lerick slid broad-
side out of the turn, spun com-
pletely around and hit the wall
directly in front of the judges
stand. Two laps later Paul
Hollister and Glen Eichman
tangled, coming out of the
same turn, and slid into the
wall. Duane Matson coming
high out of the same turn,
knicked the rear of Eichman's
car and spun both his machine
and Eichman's around. Hollis-

passed over his foot. When the

BACK-UP RACE
AT HALES CORNERS
Promoter John Kaishian is
scheduling an action packed
back-up race, as an added at-
traction, as modified stock car
racing moves into its 16th
week at Hales Corners, Satur-
day night, September 12th. The
event features strictly stock
autos racing in reverse gear
around the track in one direc-
tion, then turning around and
racing another lap in the op-
posite direction. All cars still
running after the completion of
'the event will be demolished in
the weekly demolition contest.
Heading a field of some 50
cars and drivers will be current
point leader Miles Melius of
Slinger, Wisconsin. Melius has
thus far racked up a total of
3,020 points in competition so
far this season. He will be
seeking his sixth Hales Cor-
ners win, in the 25 lap main
event that is scheduled. Other
top point leaders expected to
participate include second place
Billy Schwister of Milwaukee,
2,630 points; third place Etchie
Biertzer, Barton, 2,440 points;
