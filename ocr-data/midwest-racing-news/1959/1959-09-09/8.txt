MIDWEST RACING NEWS
CHICAGO, Sept. 6 - Frank
Lobaza, founder and director of
STAR, has announced the re-
ceipt in less than a week since
announcement of the event a
total of 14 entries for the
STAR Midwest Stock Car
Championship for late models
Sunday, October 4, at Meadow-
dale International Raceways.
This program over the ex-
citing, high speed 3.3 mile
Meadowdale course that in-
cludes the track's now famous
American version of the Monza
wall is expected to attract the
cream of late model stock car
stars and will definitely es-
tablish the midwest champion
for 1959.
Lobaza last week signed con-
tracts for the special show
with Meadowdale's president,
Leonard Besinger. The Octo-
ber 4 program will be an all-
day affair starting with time
trials in the morning. Other
preliminary events will be cli-
maxed with the running of this
first STAR Midwest Champion-
ship.
