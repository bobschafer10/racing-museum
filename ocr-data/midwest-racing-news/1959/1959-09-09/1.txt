Rathmann Rolls

WILKE TEAM
FORCED OUT
Piloting an ex-Scarab re-
named Meister Brauser No. 1 at
an average speed of 88.858
mph, Augie Pabst' of Milwau-
kee, came from behind in the
second heat of Meadowdale's
formula Libre race Sunday to
win $2,700 first prize money
before a crowd of 62,632.
Pabst also won Saturday's over
1500 CC Sports car race to
take home an additional $300
in a 2.5 Litre Ferrari.
Lloyd Ruby of Phoenix,
Arizona driving a Maserati at
an average speed of 87.403
mph, just 7 seconds behind
Pabst, finished second to win
$1,500. Third place money of
$1,100 went to John Fitch of
Lime Rock, Conn., in a Cooper
Monaco.
Rathmann, who was running
3rd place with only 5 laps re-
maining in the race, lost a
wheel and rolled over 12 times.
He suffered a moderate concus-
sion and was released shortly
after being. admitted at Sher-
man Hospital in Elgin, Ill.
Eddie Crawford of North-
brook, Ill., won the first sports
car race on Saturday in an
Elva. The second race was won
by Augie Pabst of Milwaukee
in a Ferrarri and the third race
was won by Lloyd Ruby of
Houston, Texas in a Maserati.
Each won $300 first prize
money.
