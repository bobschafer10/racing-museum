Milwaukee made it two features in a row as he won
the 50 lap modified stock car feature at state fair
park Thursday night. Schwister won the 100 lap
main race that closed out the summer racing season
in mid-August. A crowd of more than 2,500 saw the
sandy-haired youngster take the lead, on the 16th
lap and finish with a comfortable half-lap margin.
Schwister set a new 50 lap
mark of 15 minutes, 2.12 sec-
onds (49.88 miles per hour)
breaking the record of 15:15.26
set by Norm Nelson, Racine,
July 24, 1958. Schwister also
claimed the big end of the $1,-
040 purse put up by race direc-
