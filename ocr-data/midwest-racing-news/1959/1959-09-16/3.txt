Kulow Clips Competition
By Al Krause
Going into the lead on the
46th lap, Carl Kulow, Ply-
mouth, won the 50 lap modifi-
ed stock car feature at state
fair park, Thursday night,
Sept. 10. This was the final
program of the season on the
one-quarter mile dirt track and
it was only fitting that the 10
year veteran of racing should
come through to win. A crowd
of about 1,500 fans sat in low
50s temperature to see Kulow
come from 14th spot past Bay
Darnell, Deerfield, Ill. for the
front spot.
Darnell, an unknown to Beer
City, fans, had worked up a
siseable lead after capturing
the top spot from Jim Gish,
West Allis, on the 20th lap.
Kulow forced his way past Dar-
nell going into the north turn
and slipped past Greg Krie-
ger's mount stalled in the
groove" while Darnell was
forced to go around the car
from the outside. Bill Schwis-
ter, Milwaukee nipped Darnell
at the finish line by employing.
the same strategy as the two
cars went into the north turn
on the last lap.
Kulow has been a steady
competitor here at the fair
park since 1952, and won the
first modified feature race
here when the Milwaukee Stock
Car Racing Association sanc-
tioned the races on Sept. 1,
1953. Cool Carl also holds the
record for 75 laps which he
established in 1955. He was
fair park track champ in 1955
and 1956 and was Milwaukee
Stock Car champ in 1955.
Race director Tom Marchese
was gratified by the appear-
ance of 47 modified stock cars
and eight spectator automobiles.
He remarked that the drivers
were to be complimented for
the fine show they put on. Mar-
chese translated his thanks into
something more concrete as he
added more than $400 to the
prize money purse to bring it up
to $1,040.
Fuzzy Fassbender, Slinger,
won time trials with a time of
17.10 seconds (52.64 miles per
hour). Fassbender then came
on to win the fourth heat but
motor trouble finished him off
in the main race after he had
come frcm 20th (last) position
on up to seventh. Russ Sorenson,
Racine; Dan Kopshe, Milwau-
kee, and Darnell won the other
three 10 lap heat races. Kopshe
again drove a fine race and
came back to nail down second
