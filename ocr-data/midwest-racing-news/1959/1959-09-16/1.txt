First Feature

Melius Breaks Axle
WAUKEGAN, Sept. 13 -
Russ Knapton of Milwaukee
captured the 25 lap feature
event at the Waukegan Speed-
way last night, scoring his
first feature win in over two
years of racing. The former,
Milwaukee stock car Champion,
roared into the lead on the
very first lap, and was never
headed as he averaged 49.29
mph in capturing the event.
The win was not one of the
easiest, however, as Swede
Erickson of Gurnee pushed him
around the track for the entire
distance. Erickson placed sec-
ond in the event, 1/2 car length
behind Knapton.
Knapton was aided by the
breakdown of many of the top
favorites in the event. Track
point leader, Eddie Stillman,
Three time Waukegan winner,
failed to make an appearance
after blowing up his engine at
Hales Corners Saturday night.
Bob Ratajczyk, a two time
winner, who is running second
in the standings, is out for the
season due to a broken leg he
suffered at a picnic earlier in
the week. Erik Johnson, High-
land Park, blew a head gasket
in the heat and was out. Joe
Fischer of Libertyville who won
the last two events was tied up
at a late model race out of town

winner, broke an axle on the
