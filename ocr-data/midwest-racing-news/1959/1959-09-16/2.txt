N. J., and Ed Crawford of
Northfield, Ill., won Road
America's 500 mile sports car
race at Elkhart Lake, Wiscon-

new track record was set at
82.18 mph.
Three cars claimed to have
won the race and it took all of
four hours of argument and
confusion to check the scorers'
sheets and arrive at the correct
winner.

and Wuesthoff, however, was
not indicative of the quality
of their driving as they lost a
valuable 20 minutes w h en
