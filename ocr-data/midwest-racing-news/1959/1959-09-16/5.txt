Hales Corners, Sept. 12 -
Miles Melius, the flying
"mouse"" from Slinger, Wiscon-
sin scored his sixth Hales Cor-
ners victory of the season,
Saturday night, Sept. 16th, in
an action packed show which
saw one driver injured in a
spectacular accident in the
semi-feature event.
Royal Zetting of Milwaukee
was treated by the track phy-
sician and then released to his
private doctor for further treat-
ment, after he spun out on the
third lap of the semi-feature
event and was hit by oncoming
machines. He suffered a brok-
en left toe, multiple euts and
abrasions to the left leg and
arm and nose bleed.
Melius took the lead in the
feature event from
"Papa"
Chuck Mueller, of Milwaukee
on the ninth lap of the 25 lap
headline event. He was not able
to "walk away' with the race,
however, as Etchie Biertzer,
Barton, pushed him all the way.
Biertzer was the evenings fast-
est qualifier with an average
speed of 57.50 mph. Mueller
put up a good fight for a high
position in the event but lost
out when he spun out on the
19thlap, lost two laps to the
leaders and was never again in
contention. Carl Kulow, Ply-
mouth placed third, followed by
Don Schuppel, Kewaskum,
Billy Schwister, Milwaukee and
Fuzzy Fassbender of Slinger.
Melius averaged 55.79 mph in
capturing the win.
