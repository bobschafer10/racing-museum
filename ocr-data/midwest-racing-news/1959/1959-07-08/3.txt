Nat'l Championship
Drag Races
The National Championship
Drag Races, America's biggest
drag racing spectacle will be
held for the second straight
year at the Great Lakes Drag-
away at Union Grove, Wis.
The event will start Friday
July 10th, and run through
Sunday July 12th. Time trials
will be held Friday, with speed
runs Saturday afternoon and
evening, and trophy competi-
tion Sunday afternoon and
evening. Racing will begin at
9 a.m. and continue until 9 p.m.
all three days to allow the
ter mile run.
More than 40 states will be
.
represented with seven out of
the fastest dragsters in the
country competing for nation
al honors along with $500 in
U.S. Savings Bonds. Also the
world's largest trophy, stand-
ing over 7 ft. in height, will be
awarded to the first driver
who can better the present
world speed record of 185.13
m.p.h. for the timed quarter at Union Grove, Wis.

MIDWEST RACING NEWS
NEW PRESS ASSOCIATION
of newspaper, magazine, radio
2
and television reporters, who
regularly cover  the motor
sports, are organizing a new
professional press association.
With auto racing in its many
forms on the increase, the need
for a comprehensive association
becomes apparent, reported the
organizing chairman Don O'-
Reilly, Inside Auto Racing syn-
dicated newspaper columnist of
Washington, D. C.
The association is a non-
profit club, working for better
understanding between. racing
people and the press, safer and
more efficient conduct of rac-
ing, more adequate press cov-
erage of the sport and better
working press coverage of the
sport and better working press
facilities, all aimed at improv-
ing the sport and the news pro-
fessions participation.

Dake Cops
First Victory
F
Darrel Dake, often a brides-
maid, but never a bride in 1958
and 1959, finally broke the ice
in IMCA stock car action, win-
ning a 100 lap feature on the
half-mile oval of the Steele
County Free Fair at Owaton-
na, Minnesota, Sunday, June
14th.
Threatening
skies forced
cancellation of time trials and
Dake took the pole by virtue
of a drawing and a number
one finish in the opening 10-
lap heat. The second heat was
annexed by Frank Richards.
Both Dake and Richards hail
from Cedar Rapids, Iowa, and
both drive 1957 Chevrolet con-
vertibles.
