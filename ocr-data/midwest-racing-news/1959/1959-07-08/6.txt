LUTZ AGAIN
Chicago, July 3 - Bill Lutz
of Louisville, Ky., continued
on his win spree here at
O'Hare Stadium tonight as he
won another 25-lap STAR New
-Late Model feature event
with the 1957 Tom's Standard
Service Chevrolet the car dri-
ven to the track championship
last year by National champ
Fred Lorenzen.
Two earlier leaders fell by
the wayside as Lutz surged to
the front quickly after starting
11th in the 18-car field. It was
Chicago's Neil Houston who
got the lead, on the drop of
starter Art Kelley's green flag
in his 1957 Ford, but he was
then passed by Al Warfield of
Forest Park, Ill. ('56 Ford).
At the finish it was Lutz,
Gene Marmor of Schiller Park,
Ill. ('57 Chevy), Red Duvall of
Hammond, Ind. ('58 Ford),
Warfield, and Bryant Tucker
of Oak Park, Ill. ('57 Buick).
