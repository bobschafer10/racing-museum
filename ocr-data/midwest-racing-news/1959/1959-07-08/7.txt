Rockford 100 to Marmor
Rockford, Ill., July 1 - The
first 100-lap Championship for
the New-Late Model stock cars
of STAR packed 'em in here
tonight at Rockford Speedway
and it was the veteran Gene
Marmor of Schiller Park, Ill.,
who went home with the lion's
share of the special little show
purse.
Another STAR 100-lapper
has been set here for Wednes-
day night, July 15, with a Sun-
day night, July 19 rain date.
The win for Marmor in his 1957
Chevrolet was his first of the
year on the STAR circuit
though he has been a top con-
tender all the way and ranks
second in STAR point stand-
The point leader, Bill Lutz
of Louisville, Ky., looked to be
on his way to another impres-
sive victory on the very fast
Rockford Speedway high banks,
but he skidded through a slick
spot on the track while lead-
ing on lap 56 and snapped an
axle on his 1957 Chevrolet.
Trailing Marmor at the fin-
