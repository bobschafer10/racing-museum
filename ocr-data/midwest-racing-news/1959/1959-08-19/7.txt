Meadowdale to Introduce New Type
The officials of Meadowdale International Race-
ways have announced the scheduling of four thrill-
ing United States Auto Club races on September 5-6.
The program will include three separate sports car
races on Saturday. There will be a pair of 20 lap
races for cars with under 1,500 CC and between
1,500 and 3,000 CC displacement engines. Larger
cars wiwll climax the day's speed classic with a 30
lap, 99 mile feautre. On Sunday, the Carpenters-
ville track will bring to the midwest an entirely new
type race - FORMULA LIBRE. It will be run in
two heats for a total of 160 miles. This Formula
Libre race will match all kinds of the fastest racing
machines in the country in one of the most thrilling
displays of speed and danger that has ever been
witnessed. Sports cars, midgets, Grand Prix cars,
championship (Indianapolis-type cars), and even a
