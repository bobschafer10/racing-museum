Lorenzen Takes 100 Lap
Windy City Championship
By Bud Booth
Chicago, August 14 -- Defending USAC and
O'Hare Stadium late model stock car champion Fred
Lorenzen of Elmhurst, Ill., tonight added another
title to his impressive and long list, as he won
O'Hare's second annual 100 lap Chicago City Cham-
pionship before a capacity turnout.
Fred and the 1956 Nickey "Purple People Eat-
er"' Chevrolet were never in trouble as he ripped
through the grueling distance covering 400 turns in
25 miles in the record time of 27:11.27. Lorenzen
knocked over eight seconds off of the old track mark
he himself set last August 25 - 27:19.72.
