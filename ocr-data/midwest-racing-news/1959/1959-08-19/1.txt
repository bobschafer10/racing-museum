MILWAUKEE, WIS., Aug.
13 - Taking the lead on the
68th lap, Bill Schwister, Mil-
waukee went on to win the 100
lap modified stock car race
on the one-quarter mile dirt
track here at state fair park
Thursday night. A crowd of
about 5,000 saw the 23-year old
daredevil move into the top
spot after a burned out rear
axle forced Ed Stillman, Mil-
waukee to quit the race. Still-
man had enough of a point lead
and was able to stand off the
other drivers to win the sca-
son's championship and the
beautiful Bardahl Oil Co. tro-
phy.
The race could be aptly call-
ed the "Heartbreak Hundred"
Stillman had the situation well
in hand when he was forced
out. Earlier in the race Miles
Melius, Slinger, came up with
some heroies that earned the
plaudits of the crowd. Melius,
caught in a brief tangle, spun
in the south turn on the 14th
lap. He lost more than a lap
to Stillman and two other front
runners. Melius got control of
things and set sail for the
front, unlapping  himself and
