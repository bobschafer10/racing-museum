Streak Now
At Four
Unbeatable At
Sun Prairie
By Tom Bloom
Billy Wood of Kenosha con-
tinued his winning streak here
at Angell Park by taking his
fourth feature race Sunday
night before 5,000 cheering
fans. Wood, who is high point
man at the Angell Park Speed-
way, rounded out another per-
fect evening as he paced the
qualifiers with a sizzling 17.49
seconds, copped his 10 lap heat
race and had a ding-dong bat-
tle winning the action packed
10 lap six fastest event.
Gary Congdon of Whitewa-
ter, Wis., copped the second
heat race of the evening and
came home. with the checkered
flag in the thrill packed 14 car
semi-final after a bad  tangle
in the first lap with Pat Dela-
grazie of Evanston, Ill. Joe
Bergen of Vinton,Jowa was
the victor of the first heat race,
taking over on the first lap and
never relinquishing the lead.
The 25 lap feature race was
one of the most competitive
shows ever witnessed by mid-
get fans in the midwest. Owen
Snyder of Janesville, Wiscon-
sin, tooling his beautiful No.
98 Chief Automotive Special of
Madison jumped off to an early
lead, but not for long, as the
hard charging Mr. Wood came
from his last row starting po-
sition like a shot out a cannon
and hauled down the first spot
arter six laps. Snyder gave
Wood a real battle and Keith
Thomas of Madison gave Sny-
der a whale of a battle for the
second spot for '15 grueling
wheel to wheel laps to nail
down the third spot.
