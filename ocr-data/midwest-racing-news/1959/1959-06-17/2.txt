Eddie Stillman Duplicates
Hales Corners Win
Ed Stillman of -Milwaukee
scored another feature win at
the Hales Corners Speedway
Saturday night to become the
first driver of this season who
has been able to duplicate his
wins. Stillman, who averaged
54.88 M.P.H. took over the
lead on the 18th lap from Don
Sorce of West Allis. By the
20th lap, Miles Melius of Sling-
er had moved, into second posi-
tion, and Fran Seamars of Mil-
waukee had moved into third.
From here to the end of the
race, it was a three-way battle
for the lead until the checkered
flag dropped ending the race
in the same positions listed.
Sorce finished, fourth; Billy
Johnson of. Oconomowoc fifth.
Earlier in the event, season
point leader Etchie Biertzer of
Barton spun on the backstretch
causing a three-car pile-up.
Biertzer, who lost ground in the
mishap, managed to recover
and place seventh in the race.
Three cars were forced out at
this point, those beeing Paul!
Feldner, Milwaukee; Jim Gish,
West Allis; and Fuzzy Fassben
der of Slinger. Feldner tried to
avoid hitting Biertzer, spun
out and was hit hard by Gish.
Fassbender, also in the entan-
glement, smashed into the other
two cars. All three were forced
from the event with damaged
machines. An earlier challen-
ger in the event, Greg Krieger
of Milwaukee, was forced out
of the race when his car burst
into flames. The fire was ex-
tinguished by track officials
and Krieger was uninjured.
